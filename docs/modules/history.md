# History Module (`tangkhul_lib.history`)

The **History** module is a planned domain module dedicated to Tangkhul community history, ancestral origins, migration traditions, historical figures, and regional events.

---

## Planned Scope & Objectives

- **Origins and Migrations**: Document traditional oral narratives of ancestral origins, the *Makhel* dispersal tradition, and settlement across the hills of Ukhrul, Kamjong, and surrounding regions.
- **Historical Chronology**: Record significant milestones from pre-colonial village sovereignty, colonial interactions, World War I Labour Corps participation, and 20th-century cultural renaissance.
- **Toponyms & Sacred Geography**: Maintain a verified gazetteer of historical village names, sacred peaks (*Shirao Kashong*, *Khayang Peak*), rivers, and ancestral boundary stones.
- **Pioneering Figures**: Document educational pioneers, language scholars, and community leaders who contributed to Tangkhul orthography and social development.

---

- Architecture and directory structures initialized.
- Manifest schema supported in `history.jsonl`.
- Historical archives and timeline datasets will be integrated in subsequent steps.
