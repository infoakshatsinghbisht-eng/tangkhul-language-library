# Songs Module (`tangkhul_lib.songs`)

The **Songs** module is a planned domain module dedicated to preserving traditional folk songs, ceremonial hymns, seasonal festival songs, and modern musical works of the Tangkhul people.

---

## Planned Scope & Objectives

- **Folk Song Preservation**: Document traditional lyrical genres including *Luira* (seed-sowing festival songs), *Chumpha* (harvest celebration songs), war songs, romantic melodies, and lullabies.
- **Lyrical Transcriptions**: Store authenticated Tangkhul stanzas alongside line-by-line English glosses and cultural context.
- **Poetic Lexicon Extraction**: Preserve archaic, figurative, and musical terms unique to Tangkhul songcraft that are not commonly found in colloquial speech.
- **Musical & Ceremonial Metadata**: Record associated rituals, dances, and seasonal contexts in `data/manifests/songs.jsonl`.

---

## Current Status (Step 1)

- Architecture and directory structures initialized.
- Manifest schema supported in `songs.jsonl`.
- Bulk song transcription and parsing will be implemented in subsequent steps.
