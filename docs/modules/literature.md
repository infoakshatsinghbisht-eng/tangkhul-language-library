# Literature Module (`tangkhul_lib.literature`)

The **Literature** module is a planned domain module dedicated to Tangkhul oral narratives, folk tales (*Khararchan*), poetry, dramatic dialogues, and riddles.

---

## Planned Scope & Objectives

- **Folk Tales (*Khararchan*)**: Document traditional legends, animal fables, mythological creation tales, and hero sagas (such as tales of *Rapeo*, *Shimreishang & Maitonphi*).
- **Poetry & Metaphor**: Preserve Tangkhul poetic forms, figurative expressions, and ceremonial invocations.
- **Riddles & Wordplay**: Archive traditional riddles that test linguistic agility and cultural knowledge among younger generations.
- **Literary Vocabulary Extraction**: Index literary words, evocative idioms, and allegorical expressions into the unified vocabulary database.

---

## Current Status (Step 1)

- Architecture and directory structures initialized.
- Manifest schema supported in `literature.jsonl`.
- Full narrative collection and text parsing will be implemented in subsequent steps.
