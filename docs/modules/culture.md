# Culture Module (`tangkhul_lib.culture`)

The **Culture** module is a planned domain module dedicated to the documentation of Tangkhul proverbs, customs, festivals, rituals, traditional crafts, and customary governance.

---

## Planned Scope & Objectives

- **Tangkhul Proverbs (*Paotam*)**: Catalogue traditional maxims, moral teachings, and metaphors with literal translations and cultural explanations.
- **Traditional Festivals**: Document the calendar, rites, and symbolism of major festivals:
  - *Luira Phanit* (Seed-sowing festival, springtime agricultural inauguration)
  - *Chumpha* (Festival of lights and post-harvest thanksgiving)
  - *Mangkhap* (Post-transplantation rest festival)
  - *Thisham* (Feast for departing souls)
  - *Dharreo* (Autumnal thanksgiving)
- **Material Culture & Attire**: Record traditional shawls (*Haora*, *Luirim*, *Seichang*), ornaments (*Cha*, *Kongsang*), and wood carving.
- **Village Governance & Clan Customs**: Document customary legal concepts (*Phungcham*, *Hangva* elders' council) and clan lineages.
