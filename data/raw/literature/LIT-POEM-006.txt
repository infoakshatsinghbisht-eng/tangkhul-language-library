LITERARY OVERVIEW & CONTEXT:
  Contemporary lyric poem passionately advocating for the revitalization of archaic Tangkhul vocabulary and ancestral roots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL LITERARY TEXT / STANZAS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Angayung kacham thing-phangli ngalung khangachei maleimana,
  Chiyan-chikaning va-chingkha ngashan ngaphar-kazatta.
  Iramwui tuitam kakhurli shoksang-kakhui meingaláng,
  Ning-ngalungwui meikha thada kachama leishat mayondatta.

  Khararchar tui eina akhama ngala kaphara khangayei,
  Arārana pamkakhung ngalei-li ngasam-phang-kazatta;
  Tui khamatha hi kacham kacham phung-ngaleili karamtatta,
  Angayung makhanganingli meingashan kachi leimana!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGLISH POETIC TRANSLATION & SCHOLARLY RENDERING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The deep roots beneath the ancient oak tree can never be shaken by the storm,
  Through changing seasons and howling winds, they nourish the living branches.
  From the deep wells of our ancestral mother tongue, new wisdom continuously springs,
  Like glowing hearthside embers, true linguistic love burns bright and constant.

  In ancestral idioms and melodic phrases, new generations find their voice,
  Dwelling upon the selfsame highlands where the forebears walked of old;
  These noble words shall echo across our mountain ridges without end,
  For without deep roots, no tree can withstand the tests of time!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LITERARY THEMES & MOTIFS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Linguistic rootedness and language preservation (*Tui Angayung*)
  * Metaphor of the mountain oak and deep roots surviving storms
  * Intergenerational transmission of mother tongue idioms
  * Archaic vocabulary as a living sanctuary of community soul

STRUCTURE & POETIC METER: Modern lyrical verse in two eight-line stanzas with regular assonance and philosophical reflection.

CRITICAL APPRECIATION & COMMENTARY:
  Title poem from the acclaimed contemporary poetry collection *Angayung* by Hunphun educator and poet Iknguira L. Shimray. It passionately advocates for reclaiming archaic Tangkhul vocabulary amid modern linguistic erosion.
