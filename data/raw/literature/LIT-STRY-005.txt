LITERARY OVERVIEW & CONTEXT:
  Pastoral realism short story following a young herdsman at dawn upon the high grasslands of Phangrei, meditating on modern changes and ancestral attachment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL LITERARY TEXT / STANZAS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Phangrei kaphung khangachei-wui akhama kashungli, mayarnao Wungnao kaphung yarsho-li seikhur mayonzatta.
  Chitharan Shirui Kashong eina Mapithel phungkhui-li meikharor zatta eina ngashan mirin kacham-kacham theikhuira.

  Seipam khangacheili mayon-kazat Wungnaona, changvei eina phanung zattada ngalei katharao wui tui phaning-nganung.
  'Kaphung ram kachamnao hi i-wui mirinwui ning-ngashang, ram-leishat mashukhamakha mirin kachiphun leimana.'

  Ukhrul town-li khangatei kazat miyaongao theilaga, Wungnaona kaphung wui pine thingwon eina ngayirwon kachon shanching.
  Phangrei wui akhama kazingmei thada shoklaga, kaphung miyaonao mirinli ngathang kazing wui mathikashung phora.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGLISH POETIC TRANSLATION & SCHOLARLY RENDERING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  As early dawn breaks over the undulating highland plateaus of Phangrei, young herdsman Wungnao walks the high ridge.
  Looking out toward the distant crags of Mount Shirui and Mapithel, blankets of white mist slowly dissolve in the sunrise.

  Guiding grazing cattle across the lush high-altitude pasturelands, he leans upon his mountain staff and contemplates his heritage.
  'These windswept ridges are the cradle of our soul; without devotion to this mountain soil, what meaning has life?'

  Though modern currents pull youth toward bustling commercial valleys, Wungnao remains anchored to pine fragrances and orchid blossoms.
  The morning light upon Phangrei shines like celestial grace, renewing the youthful herdsman's dedication to his ancestral land.

LITERARY THEMES & MOTIFS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Pastoral contemplation and rural mountain tranquility (*Phangrei Akhama*)
  * Tension between rapid modern urbanization and traditional pastoral vocations
  * Highland ecosystem awareness: pine forests, mountain pastures, and cliffside water sources
  * Youth identity anchored in ancestral land stewardship and spiritual groundedness

STRUCTURE & POETIC METER: Lyrical prose narrative structured with poetic interior monologues and pastoral vignettes.

CRITICAL APPRECIATION & COMMENTARY:
  Authored by Pamkhuila Shaiza, this short story exemplifies modern Tangkhul realistic fiction. It portrays the quiet dignity of rural highland youth resisting alienation by finding spiritual purpose in pastoral work and ecological intimacy.
