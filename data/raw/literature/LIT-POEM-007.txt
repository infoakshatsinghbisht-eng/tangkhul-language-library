LITERARY OVERVIEW & CONTEXT:
  Evocative pastoral poem portraying the spiritual innocence, fireside education, and loom artistry of Tangkhul maidens.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL LITERARY TEXT / STANZAS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ngalā mirin khamatha, chiyan kashungwui won thada,
  Luirim chonkashan shanda shanaonao ning-ngachei kashung.
  Kong-khamatha-wui tuitam eina shanaowui ning-kacham,
  Phungcham ramwui phangyāngli laa seiser-kazatta.

  Meiphung-li pamda avawui tui theida khangarang,
  Kashan-kachon eili arārawui ngashan thada kapikathat;
  Ngalā mirin hi Tangkhul shanao-wui khamashung shakshen,
  Meingaláng khon eina ngaleili mayon-kazatta!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGLISH POETIC TRANSLATION & SCHOLARLY RENDERING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The life and grace of a maiden, blooming like wild lilies when spring returns,
  Draped in the proud woven stripes of the Luirim shawl, her heart remains pure.
  Like the clear waters of mountain streams, her spirit reflects innocence and wisdom,
  Singing folk ballads across the terraced fields of Phungcham village.

  By the warm fireside she absorbs the gentle counsel of her mother,
  Weaving the ancestral patterns of her clan upon the rhythmic backstrap loom;
  The life of a maiden embodies the sacred poise of Tangkhul womanhood,
  Shining with inner light across the high mountain ridges!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LITERARY THEMES & MOTIFS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Poetic celebration of Tangkhul maidenhood (*Ngalā Mirin*)
  * Customary filial respect, fireside instruction, and domestic grace
  * The loom as an instrument of feminine artistic expression
  * Mountain stream and wild blossom metaphors for spiritual purity

STRUCTURE & POETIC METER: Eight-line pastoral lyric with parallel metric structure and evocative sensory imagery.

CRITICAL APPRECIATION & COMMENTARY:
  A standout lyrical piece by Iknguira L. Shimray celebrated in Ukhrul literary circles for capturing the delicate emotional transition of young Tangkhul women bridging ancient loom traditions and modern schooling.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY TANGKHUL POETIC & LITERARY VOCABULARY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Ngalā mirin            : The life, journey, and customary passage of an unmarried young woman
  * Ning-kacham            : Purity of conscience and upright modesty
  * Kong-khamatha          : Pure, unpolluted crystal mountain stream
  * Shakshen               : Poise, graceful demeanor, exemplary character
