LITERARY OVERVIEW & CONTEXT:
  Seminal patriotic poem celebrating the alpine hills, ethical uprightness, and sacred heritage of the Tangkhul territory, widely taught as a cultural anthem.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL LITERARY TEXT / STANZAS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tangkhul ngalei khamatha, kaphung-yarsho mayon-kazing,
  Iramwui ngalung kacham, leikashi eina ngasothong.
  Shirui wonna kaphungli khamatha eina mayondatta,
  Kazing chiyan kashungli kazeiram thada ringkapha.

  I-ava ram, i-arāra ram, nawui mang eili pamkapeiya,
  Mayarnao khangarang, shanaonao khamatha mayon-ser,
  Khamashung tui eina ngashan mirin kasak Tangkhul ram,
  Thithang thithang nawui ming phung-ngathang kazingli khaláng-fara!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ENGLISH POETIC TRANSLATION & SCHOLARLY RENDERING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  O beautiful land of the Tangkhul, crowned by soaring mountain ridges,
  The sacred soil of our ancestors, flourishing in enduring love and harmony.
  The Shirui Lily blooms in unmatched splendor upon the crests,
  When morning mists ascend, it mirrors the serene beauty of the heavens.

  Land of my mother, land of my ancestral fathers, in your embrace I find rest,
  Where brave sons stand vigilant and fair daughters walk in graceful poise,
  Bound by upright truth and righteous customs, O beloved Tangkhul soil,
  Through all the ages, your sacred name shall soar high above the mountain peaks!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LITERARY THEMES & MOTIFS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Patriotic devotion and veneration of the ancestral homeland (*Ava Ram Leishat*)
  * Poetic iconography of Mount Shirui and the endemic Shirui Lily (*Shirui Won*)
  * Moral celebration of upright customary truth (*Khamashung Tui*)
  * Hymnodic cadence and national cultural affirmation

STRUCTURE & POETIC METER: Eight-line patriotic lyric ode composed in octosyllabic rhyming quatrains with solemn choral resonance.

CRITICAL APPRECIATION & COMMENTARY:
  Composed by the esteemed poet Y.K. Shimray and featured in *Tangkhul Poetry: Book 1*. Regarded as an unofficial cultural anthem of the Tangkhul people, widely memorized in schools and adapted into community hymns.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY TANGKHUL POETIC & LITERARY VOCABULARY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Tangkhul Ngalei        : The ancestral homeland / territory of the Tangkhul Naga people
  * I-arāra ram            : The land of our remote ancestors / sacred forefathers
  * Ringkapha              : Joyful, flourishing life lived in peace and cultural wholeness
  * Phung-ngathang         : Above the sentinel mountain summits / eternal height
