ENGLISH POETIC TRANSLATION & SCHOLARLY RENDERING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Tangkhul Khararchan: Canonical Folktale Reader Vol. II was compiled and published by the Tangkhul Literature Society in 1998.
  This volume anthologizes over 30 authentic folk narratives, animal fables, clan legends, and customary parables from across the region.
  Compiled under the editorship of Kanrei Shaiza and native scholars consulting elders across Phungcham, Chingjaroi, and Kamjong.
  Each narrative carefully documents dialectal terminology, archaic grammatical inflections, and contextual moral proverbs.
  Adopted as a foundational canonical textbook for secondary and collegiate education throughout Manipur's hill districts.
  This publication represents a crowning institutional milestone in the systematic textual preservation of Tangkhul oral heritage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LITERARY THEMES & MOTIFS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Comprehensive anthologization of Tangkhul folk narratives and ancestral legends
  * Standardization of indigenous narrative orthography without erasing dialectal markers
  * Curricular integration of oral literature in formal Tangkhul language education
  * Institutional stewardship of cultural patrimony by the Tangkhul Literature Society

STRUCTURE & POETIC METER: Multi-genre anthological compendium with standardized pedagogical glosses, moral commentaries, and study questions.

CRITICAL APPRECIATION & COMMENTARY:
  Edited by Kanrei Shaiza and published by the Tangkhul Literature Society (TLS). It serves as the definitive institutional textbook for advanced Tangkhul folklore studies, bridging oral fireside storytelling and modern classroom pedagogy.
