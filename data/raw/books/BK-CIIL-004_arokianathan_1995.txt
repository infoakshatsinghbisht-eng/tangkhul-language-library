Published in Mysore under the CIIL Dictionary Series (140 pages, 1995).
This trilingual reference manual provides systematic lexical entries for Tangkhul root morphemes,
compounds, derived forms, grammatical word-classes, English definitions, and Hindi equivalents,

## Chapter 1: Lexicographical Conventions (Page 4)
### Chapter 1: Phonological Structure & Conventions
CHAPTER 1: PHONOLOGICAL STRUCTURE & LEXICOGRAPHICAL CONVENTIONS
Summary: Vowel length (a vs ā), stop series, tone marks, and trilingual alignment.

LEXICOGRAPHICAL FORMAT:
Entry format: Headword (Tangkhul) [Part of Speech] English Definition / Hindi Equivalent (हिंदी अर्थ).
Example: sairik [noun] alphabet, letter / वर्ण, अक्षर.
Minimal vowel length pairs: 'sa' [verb] to do / करना; 'sā' [noun] animal, flesh / पशु, माँस.

## Chapter 3: Verb Conjugation Table (Page 9)
### Chapter 3 Verb Suffixes & Conjugation
CHAPTER 3 STUDY GUIDE & VERB CONJUGATION
TENSE SUFFIXES:
  - Present: -ei / -i ('i sai' = I do)
  - Past: -ser / -da ('i sada' = I did / have done)
  - Future: -ra ('i sara' = I will do)
  - Imperative: -lu ('salu!' = do!)
