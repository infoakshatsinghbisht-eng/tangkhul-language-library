PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Yarra celebrates the age-grade youth institutions (Longshim and Yarra)
that formed the social and moral backbone of traditional Tangkhul
society. The novel captures the humor, musical creativity, and peer
solidarity that flourished in these dormitory fellowships.
Archival Catalog Reference: TCC-TKH-NOVEL-YARRA

CHAPTER 1: THE LONGSHIM FELLOWSHIP (YOUTH DORMITORY LIFE)
Summary: Life inside the traditional Tangkhul dormitory where storytelling, oral lore, an...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Ngazing shoklakha, village-wui yaronaobingna Longshimli
(youth dormitory) pamkazipsaye. Eldersbingna athumli ancestral wari,
customary laws, kala archery training miye. Tingteila kala siphung flute
kakhong eina athumna traditional laa tuitam khuiye. Longshim china
youth-li discipline kala clan solidarity kasakakhui shim thada leisai.
English Translation / Meaning:
At nightfall, village youths gathered in the Longshim (communal youth
dormitory). Elders instructed them in ancestral chronicles, customary
laws, and the art of archery. With tambourines and bamboo flutes, they
rehearsed traditional folk songs. The Longshim served as a sacred
academy of character, discipline, and communal solidarity.

CHAPTER 2: THE COURTSHIP SONGS OF SPRING (YARRA LAA)
Summary: Antiphonal courtship singing during the clearing of agricultural paths....
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: Zingyur atamli, mayarnaobing kala ngalanaobingna shongfa
shomlakha Yarra Laa honserie. Mayarnaobingna call verses honsanglaga,
ngalanaobingna response verses mathat-eina ngahanmi. Tuiwon kaphā eina
athum romantic affection chitheiye; kha customary modesty (Kashan)
kuppup preserve saye.
English Translation / Meaning:
In the spring season, as young men and women cleared the mountain
footpaths together, they sang the antiphonal Yarra songs. The youths
launched poetic call verses, and the maidens responded in graceful
rhyming counterpoint. Through elegant metaphors they conveyed romantic
admiration while preserving strict customary modesty.

CHAPTER 3: THE HARVEST FEAST & LIFELONG BONDS
Summary: The youth celebrate the harvest festival, transitioning into responsible adult h...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: Chumpha harvest festival shoklakha, Yarra group-na village-li
community service salaga feast kasaye. Mayarnaobing kala ngalanaobingna
marriage eina household kathara phokserie. Longshimwui friendship thot
pupup athum lungli leirap sara.
English Translation / Meaning:
When the Chumpha harvest festival arrived, the youth association
rendered community service and held a celebratory feast. Betrothed
couples stepped forward into honorable marriage and established new
households. The deep bonds forged in the Longshim remained unbroken
throughout their adult lives.
