PREFACE TO THE HISTORIC 1927 EDITION
The completion of the New Testament in Tangkhul Naga represents thirty years
of dedicated translation, commencing in Ukhrul in 1896. With the generous
collaboration of Tangkhul native elders, pastors, and scholars, each book
was painstakingly revised against the original Greek manuscripts.
Published by the British & Foreign Bible Society at the Baptist Mission Press,
Calcutta, 1927. This edition provided the foundation for Tangkhul literacy,
hymnody, and 20th-century standard prose style.

CHAPTER 1: MATTHEW KAHONA KASA: THE NATIVITY & THE BEATITUDES
Summary: The genealogy of Christ and the historic Sermon on the Mount Beatitudes rendered...
Tangkhul Sacred Text:
Matthew 5:3-10: Mangazingli ngachamkakhui bingna mathasek-u, kasakei
kazingram athumwuisai. Khangaringli ngachirbingna mathasek-u, kasakei
athumli khangasak milu sara. Khamanoubingna mathasek-u, kasakei athum
mikumo ngaleili ngashansara. Mashung-kasa vang kachamkashi bingna
mathasek-u, athumna thingkhalara. Khangacham thotli ngashanbingna
mathasek-u, athumli ngashan milu sara.
English Translation:
Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted. Blessed are
the meek: for they shall inherit the earth. Blessed are they which do
hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled. Blessed
are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.

CHAPTER 1 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Lexical Terms:
  * mangazing (noun): spirit, soul, inner mind
    Example: Mangazingli kaphā.
  * kazingram (noun): heaven, sky realm, celestial kingdom
    Example: Kazingramwui ot.
  * mashung-kasa (noun): righteousness, justice, upright conduct
    Example: Mashung-kasa khayang.
Grammar Notes:
  - Suffix -bing marks nominal plurality (ngachirbing = those who mourn).
  - Optative imperative/blessing suffix -sek-u expresses solemn blessing ('mathasek-u' = blessed be).

CHAPTER 2: JOHN KAHONA KASA: THE INCARNATION & THE LIGHT OF MEN
Summary: Prologue of John in classic Tangkhul syntax, exploring the theological rendering...
Tangkhul Sacred Text:
Kaphung-kachungwui thotli Tui li-li-sai, kala Tuichi Varivarali lei,
kala Tuichi Varivarasai. Chiya kaphung-kachungli Varivarawui kachungli
leisai. Ali vangteina ot kathata pamei sakahaisai; kala sachitheikahai
thotwui tharili A matheida khikha masasa mana. Ali ringkapha lei, kala
ringkapha china mikumowui mangashan phāsa-e. Kala mangashan chi
ngashingli ngashane, kala ngashingna chi mathakakhui-mana.
English Translation:
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word
was God. The same was in the beginning with God. All things were made by
him; and without him was not any thing made that was made. In him was
life; and the life was the light of men. And the light shineth in
darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.

CHAPTER 2 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Lexical Terms:
  * kaphung-kachung (noun): the very beginning, dawn of creation
    Example: Kaphung-kachung eina.
  * ringkapha (noun): life, vitality, eternal animation
    Example: Ringkapha wui Tui.
  * mangashan (noun): light, luminescence, enlightenment
    Example: Mangashan china ngashane.
Grammar Notes:
  - Reduplication 'li-li-sai' indicates continuous remote past existence.
  - Negative verbal frame 'ma-...-mana' in 'mathakakhui-mana' (did not comprehend/overcome).

CHAPTER 3: THE ACTS OF THE APOSTLES: PENTECOST & THE FIRST FELLOWSHIP
Summary: The descent of the Holy Spirit and early communal living described in Ukhrul dia...
Tangkhul Sacred Text:
Acts 2:1-4: Pentecostwui phanit atamli athumpup ngalem akhali
ngaburkapam leisai. Chitharan khamathān kazingwui eina hu-hu kaho
khongyui kakhangahui akhata rarer, kala athum pamkapam shim tharili
chuiserie. Kala athumli meikazat theirei katha tuitak thada kashikaza
theiser, kala athum akhakha wui tuingalungli pamserie. Kala athumpup
Kazing Mangazing Katharana phāserlaga, kapei tui katek-tek eina tui
khangayei honserie.
English Translation:
When the day of Pentecost was fully come, they were all with one accord
in one place. And suddenly there came a sound from heaven as of a
rushing mighty wind, and it filled all the house where they were
sitting. And there appeared unto them cloven tongues like as of fire,
and it sat upon each of them. And they were all filled with the Holy
Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues.

CHAPTER 3 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Lexical Terms:
  * ngaburkapam (verb): to sit gathered in one assembly
    Example: Athumpup ngaburkapam leisai.
  * khongyui (noun): mighty sound, rumbling voice
    Example: Khongyui kakhangahui.
  * katek-tek (adj): diverse, differing, foreign
    Example: Tui katek-tek.
Grammar Notes:
  - Simile construction 'katha' (like, as of) in 'meikazat theirei katha' (like tongues of flame).
  - Completive plural verbal aspect '-ser' used repeatedly across clauses.

CHAPTER 4: ROMANS 8: LIFE IN THE SPIRIT & INSEPARABLE LOVE
Summary: St. Paul's exposition of spiritual victory and the hymn of divine love in Tangkh...
Tangkhul Sacred Text:
Romans 8:31-39: Vareva ithui ngachaili li-daran, khipana ithuli
khangatei sara? Athishur awui Naomayārli mathayala ithupup wui vang
mikahaina, A eina shurda khikha mamishukra kachi lei? Khipana ithuli
Christawui khangayei eina kashit sara? Ringkaphashi kaji-ra, mathikaji-
ra, kazingkaphung-ra? Mano, ot hithatha tharili ithumli ngarukahai A
eina ithumna pamei khangaraorap khui-kaphāe.
English Translation:
If God be for us, who can be against us? He that spared not his own Son,
but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely
give us all things? Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall
tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or
peril, or sword? Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors
through him that loved us.

CHAPTER 4 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Lexical Terms:
  * khangayei (noun): love, affection, sacred charity
    Example: Christawui khangayei.
  * ngarukahai (verb/adj): beloved, held precious
    Example: Ithumli ngarukahai.
  * khangaraorap (noun): triumph, overwhelming victory
    Example: Khangaraorap khui-kaphā.
Grammar Notes:
  - Rhetorical interrogative particle '-ra' forming existential questions.
  - Postposition 'eina' marking agentive/instrumental cause of triumph.

CHAPTER 5: 1 CORINTHIANS 13: THE HYMN OF CHRISTIAN LOVE (KHANGAYEIWUI LAA)
Summary: The canonical lyrical translation of 1 Corinthians 13 celebrating enduring love....
Tangkhul Sacred Text:
1 Corinthians 13:1-8: I mikumo kala kashang kazingwui tui eina hang-
khalangli, iwui tuingalungli khangayei malei-kaji tharan, i
khongkhanghui tui katha thada kasaye. Khangayeina khangachon lei,
ningkazang lei; khangayeina hingkamachur malei-mana; khangayeina aphasa
mathaikhangasang malei-mana, ningkazao malei-mana. Khangayeina
kachikathāna khikhali mamatung-mana; tui-ngashungli mathat-e. Chitharan
chotkhangashit (faith), ngachikan (hope), khangayei (love) hi kashiko
li-leirap sara; chawui ngachaili kaphāmeina khangayeisai.
English Translation:
Though I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, and have not
charity, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal. Charity
suffereth long, and is kind; charity envieth not; charity vaunteth not
itself, is not puffed up. Doth not behave itself unseemly, seeketh not
her own, is not easily provoked, thinketh no evil; Rejoiceth not in
iniquity, but rejoiceth in the truth. And now abideth faith, hope,
charity, these three; but the greatest of these is charity.

--- Page 17 ---
CHAPTER 5 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Lexical Terms:
  * ningkazang (adj): patient, enduring of heart, forbearing
    Example: Ningkazang mi.
  * hingkamachur (noun): envy, jealousy, resentment
    Example: Hingkamachur malei.
  * chotkhangashit (noun): faith, spiritual trust, conviction
    Example: Chotkhangashit kashap.
Grammar Notes:
  - Compound nominalization: ning (heart/mind) + kazang (deep/enduring) = patience.
  - Superlative suffix -mei attached to adjective: kaphāmei (greatest/best).

CHAPTER 6: REVELATION 21-22: THE NEW JERUSALEM & THE RIVER OF LIFE
Summary: The apocalyptic vision of the New Heaven and New Earth in vivid Tangkhul descrip...
Tangkhul Sacred Text:
Revelation 21:1-4: Kala i kazing kathara kala ngalei kathara theiserie;
kasakei kazing khare kala ngalei khare chi ngapaoritserie, kala
ngashakazil chi maleilu-mana. Kala i shungkhara shillong kathara,
Jerusalem Kathara, Kazingram eina Varivarawui pam eina yorura kapik
theiserie. Kala i kaphungli khongyui kakhangahui akhata kapei theiser:
'Kazingramwui shim mikumoli li-leisara!' Kala Varevana athum mikwui
mukthi thapup luithatsara; chitharan kathiri maleira, ngachiri maleira,
kashamkashi maleilu-mana; kasakei thot khare chi ngapaoritserie.
English Translation:
And I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the
first earth were passed away; and there was no more sea. And I John saw
the holy city, new Jerusalem, coming down from God out of heaven,
prepared as a bride adorned for her husband. And God shall wipe away all
tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow,
nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things
are passed away.

--- Page 20 ---
CHAPTER 6 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Lexical Terms:
  * shillong (noun): citadel, metropolis, fortified holy city
    Example: Jerusalem Shillong.
  * mukthi (noun): tears, weeping drops
    Example: Mukthi luithat.
  * ngapaorit (verb): to pass away completely, vanish, be transformed
    Example: Ot khare chi ngapaorit.
Grammar Notes:
  - Future negative modal '-lu-mana' denoting absolute cessation of pain.
  - Cessative verbal compound 'luithat' (to wipe away cleanly).
