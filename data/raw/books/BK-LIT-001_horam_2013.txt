## BK-LIT-001 -- Preface & Synopsis
### Preface & Synopsis of the Book
PREFACE & SYNOPSIS BY DR. RINGKAHAO HORAM:
'For the Tangkhul, song is a way of life, history, and belief system.'
This monumental compendium contains 161 traditional folk poems and songs (Haolaa)
collected from across the 250 Tangkhul villages. It preserves varieties of songs
sung at particular seasons, agricultural events, bedtime, feasts of merit, and mourning,
safeguarding irreplaceable Tangkhul oral literature for future generations.

## Chapter 1: Creation & Migration Songs (Page 4)
### Chapter 1: Creation, Origins & Ancestral Migration Songs
CHAPTER 1: CREATION, ORIGINS & ANCESTRAL MIGRATION SONGS
Song 1: Miurlung, Yangyirlaa  |  Song 2: Miurlung Kathur Laa  |  Song 43: Origin of Tangkhul

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Oh! hauri haura kachida haulu? Oh! hauri haura wahukongda haowa, Oh! wahukongda tunga (Hwang-Ho River).
O Kachili Tunglo Awa Samshokli Tunga, O Makungana ili phunga, O Makunganao ili pangthan,
Lungshang makang ana safasing sing, Mala mangli Hunphun Ava kharar.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Where did the ancient ancestors emerge? From the river valley of Wahukong (Hwang-Ho) they came.
From Samsok in the east our lineage migrated; Makungana sustained and guided us on the mountain trails;
In Hunphun the revered ancestral founders struck their staves and lit the ancient hearth.

## Chapter 2: Agricultural Cycles (Page 6)
### Chapter 2: Agricultural Cycles: Jhumming, Planting & Harvest
CHAPTER 2: AGRICULTURAL CYCLES: JHUMMING, PLANTING & HARVEST
Song 13: Zingkum Luivat Laa  |  Song 16: Luikasom Laa  |  Song 17: Mahat Laa

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Kongrei mathei kongrei, kongrei shailo marei salo; Matei kachui manoye, Mala ifu heinao naoye.
Ithanvara iram leisaye, Isu luisa khinasa, ima lera khinasa; Maleira souralo, isa luisa lung haorana.
Zingkum leiyao leiyameirano, chungmeida kharshokrano, phungshon phungthaigei.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
The seeds are sown, let the terrace waters flow; beloved companions, transplant the green shoots in rows.
Sweet is the labor on our sunny hillsides; who will eat my savory buffalo meat, who will drink our aged rice beer?
May the harvest be tenfold and granaries overflow with golden grain for the new year.

## Chapter 3: Morung & Dormitory Life (Page 8)
### Chapter 3: Village Morung & Dormitory Life Songs
CHAPTER 3: VILLAGE MORUNG (LONGRA) & DORMITORY LIFE SONGS
Song 38: I Longnao (Morung Drinking Song)  |  Song 108: Meisumlaa

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Leader: I longnao (I Longra) ... ho...o...o!  Members: Rapamlu ... ho...o...o!
Machapralu ... ho...o...o! Sairam samjiya, sairam samjiya!
Oh longra makazangwo, nawui shimli khisapamlo? Thakta razanglo, thakta razanglo!

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Comrades of the Longra (dormitory), gather together! Sit around the hearth and let no tears fall!
Why sit solitary in your home? Come swiftly into the warmth and fellowship of the Morung!

## Chapter 4: Historical Ballads & Wars (Page 10)
### Chapter 4: Historical Ballads: World War I & Regional Conflicts
CHAPTER 4: HISTORICAL BALLADS: WORLD WAR I & REGIONAL CONFLICTS
Song 21: France Khavawui Laa (1917-18)  |  Song 22: Khongjai Rai Khavawui Laa (1919)

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Rai ramli khava, rai ramli khava yaronpa, Yaronpa nakhi vasalo? Iya mavangai, sayisa sayi na tanti sahaowa.
Franceshi ram khava, Franceli raikhavai; Makuiye makuiye, pham kata hiyo; Wungasam chap ngacha, rai ram khavayo.
Ah khongjai rai khava yaronpa yaronva, Sangna ram penga yaronva; Kuiwung kuivaya suphung mei.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Young warriors journeying to the distant war across the ocean to France in 1917;
Why must our bravest youth leave their native hills? Bitter tears fell upon the red mountain trails.
In foreign battlefields they stood tall, carrying the courage and honor of the Tangkhul nation.

## Chapter 5: Romance & Love Songs (Page 12)
### Chapter 5: Romance, Courtship & Love Songs (Leishat Laa)
CHAPTER 5: ROMANCE, COURTSHIP & LOVE SONGS (LEISHAT LAA)
Song 19: Leishat Laa  |  Song 44: Nana Kanta Singloo  |  Song 64: Oh Iwui Sakhangai Aluinao

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Nana Kanta singloo, Ina Khailan singda, Kongli alei shonsa yahoo!
Oh iwui sakhangai aluinao, kathuili oja saleiya aluinao; Mayo kachang tarn rahaira aluinao.
Oh ini lungchang malai haosa, phaningda makhui aluinao leishiya; Kazingli samphangluinaosa aluinao.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Take up your woven fishing net, and I shall take my line; together let us cross the rushing mountain stream!
Beloved of my soul, though earthly circumstances separate our paths, my heart remembers your gentle eyes.
In heaven above, our souls shall meet again, free from the sorrows of this fleeting world.

## Chapter 6: Festival Dances & Virgin Songs (Page 14)
### Chapter 6: Festival Dances & Virgin Songs (Laa Khanganui)
CHAPTER 6: FESTIVAL DANCES & VIRGIN SONGS (LAA KHANGANUI)
Song 46: Luira Sangai Uwa  |  Song 85: Laa Nganuilaa  |  Song 86: Raphei Laa Nganuila

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Luira sangai uwa, hoksa sangai uwa; Awungo Awunga shangrei rukchang ura, Yarui rukthang Kazing mathang ngaiyijile.
Zingkam won wonluiya, ngalanao mawonluipaiya; Ngalanaowa mawon luipaiya, nathanram khavasangmeiya.
Phaoli chan ma-ungkhararyo, haoriva haorava leiretnasaya.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
The Luira festival dawns, the sacrificial feast is prepared; the Awunga and elders enter the sacred circle.
Youth blossoms like the wildflowers of the hills only once; seize the bright days of innocence and dance,
For time flows unreturning like the mountain brook, and youth departs upon the winds.

## Chapter 7: Laments & Memorials (Page 16)
### Chapter 7: Laments, Funerary Memorials & Philosophy
CHAPTER 7: LAMENTS, FUNERARY MEMORIALS & PHILOSOPHY
Song 37: Ili Vaiphumlakha  |  Song 41: Khamathan Longli

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Ili vaiphumlakha, Oh Ava khi mang shimanlo? Avayo khi mang shimanlo? Lengshongthei shimanna, Mayarnao mangna.
Khamathan longli yurkathan masaralu, Ngaleizingli kapam sangmeiya (kazeiram);
Khamna longli khangkaser masaralu, Ngaleizingli khyip sangmeiya.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
When sorrow buried me in darkness, what beloved dream was lost? The golden acorn fell from the branch in its prime.
Boast not of riches and pride in this transitory life; beneath the earth in Kazeiram we all sleep as equals;
Neither pride nor arrogance endures beyond the grave; live uprightly in truth and honor.

TRADITIONAL FORMS OF TANGKHUL MOURNING (CHAPZATLAA):
  1. Chapzatlaa: Spontaneous lament chanted over the departed relative.

## Chapter 8: Patriotic Anthems (Page 19)
### Chapter 8: Patriotic Anthems & Tribal Identity
CHAPTER 8: PATRIOTIC ANTHEMS & TRIBAL IDENTITY
Song 47: Tangkhul Ramphat  |  Song 53: Tangkhul Avaram

TANGKHUL POETIC TEXT:
Ngashan yanglo hey, Tangkhul ngalei hi! Kaphung kapha kongrei kongra hey, Khipana phashok Tangkhul ram? Varewui sokkhami ramna.
Oh Tangkhul avaram, Tangkhul sinaram! Khangayam machining Irong sampunram, Ham kasa ram chiya Longpi ram na saya;
Khamakang shim savai Hungpungram cement, Shirui ram lily mavatma nanaobing! Ringpha meiranu, mahomeiranu!

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Behold our glorious homeland, this beloved Tangkhul soil! Mountain peaks and crystal streams, a divine gift of God.
Beloved Tangkhul motherland! Land of peace and industry: Longpi crafting the timeless black pottery;
Hungpung building solid walls, and Shirui Kashung blooming with the peerless Shirui Lily! May peace and honor reign forever!
