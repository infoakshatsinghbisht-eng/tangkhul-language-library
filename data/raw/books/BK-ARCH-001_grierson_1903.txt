## Linguistic Survey of India: Specimens of the Bodo, Nāgā, and Kachin Groups (Tangkhul Section) -- Preface & Imprint
### Preface & Archival Information
This volume constitutes the official Tangkhul section of the Linguistic
Survey of India (Vol. III, Part II), surveyed by Sir George Abraham
Grierson and printed at the Office of the Superintendent of Government
Printing, Calcutta, in 1903. It preserves the earliest comprehensive
comparative grammatical analysis, standard vocabulary list, and translated
textual specimens of the Tangkhul Naga language as spoken in the Ukhrul
subdivision of Manipur.

## Chapter 1: Introduction & Dialectal Classification (Page 4)
### Chapter 1: Introduction & Dialectal Classification
CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION & DIALECTAL CLASSIFICATION
Summary: Geographical distribution, tribal divisions, and linguistic position in the Tibeto-Burman family.

TANGKHUL READING TEXT:
Tangkhul Naga hi Manipur ramwui kaphung zanshorli kapam tui thotna.
Ukhrul kaphung hi Tangkhul tuiwui phungreitang thada shiyan kashakna.
Tui hi Tibeto-Burman wui Naga-Kuki tui ngachaili shangserna.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
The Tangkhul Nagas inhabit the eastern hill tracts of Manipur State. The
village of Ukhrul serves as the central cultural and linguistic
standard. The language belongs to the Naga-Bodo / Tibeto-Burman
linguistic stock.

## Chapter 2: Phonology and the Roman Alphabet (Page 6)
### Chapter 2: Phonology and the Roman Alphabet
CHAPTER 2: PHONOLOGY AND THE ROMAN ALPHABET
Summary: Vowels, consonants, diphthongs, and glottal tone stops introduced by W. Pettigrew.

TANGKHUL READING TEXT:
Vowels: a, ā, e, ē, i, ī, o, ō, u, ū. Consonants: k, kh, ng, ch, chh, j,
t, th, n, p, ph, m, y, r, l, w, s, sh, h. Kakhalat kashak shiyan eina
tui khangatei kachung lei.

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Vowel inventory with phonemic length distinctions: short and long
vowels. Consonantal series with aspirated stops (kh, th, ph, chh).
Syllable-final consonants and tonal inflections alter word
significations.

## Chapter 3: The Noun System & Case Inflections (Page 8)
### Chapter 3: The Noun System & Case Inflections
CHAPTER 3: THE NOUN SYSTEM & CASE INFLECTIONS
Summary: Natural gender indicators, plural formations, and postpositional case endings.

TANGKHUL READING TEXT:
Tuiwon bing: mayarnaona (nominative), mayarnaoli (accusative/dative),
mayarnaowui (genitive), mayarnaoeina (instrumental/ablative). Chitharan
shiyan kakhane: si (dog), siva (bitch), simayār (male dog).

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Case suffixes attached to nominal stems: -na (nominative/agentive), -li
(dative/objective), -wui (genitive/possessive), -eina
(instrumental/ablative). Gender markers: -va (female) and -mayār (male).

## Chapter 4: Adjectives, Numerals & Pronouns (Page 10)
### Chapter 4: Adjectives, Numerals & Pronouns
CHAPTER 4: ADJECTIVES, NUMERALS & PRONOUNS
Summary: Adjectival prefix ka-, degrees of comparison, cardinal numerals, and pronoun paradigms.

TANGKHUL READING TEXT:
Kaphā (good), kakhui (bitter), kacham (poor). Comparison: kaphāmei
(better), pamei (best). Numerals: akha (1), khani (2), kathum (3), mati
(4), phangā (5), tharuk (6), shini (7), chishat (8), chiko (9), thakharā
/ magā (10). Pronouns: i (I), na (thou), a (he/she).

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Descriptive adjectives are derived using prefix ka-. Comparison is
marked with -mei. Cardinal numerals from 1 to 10. Personal pronouns: 1st
person 'i', 2nd person 'na', 3rd person 'a'; plurals: ithum, nathum,
athum.

## Chapter 5: Verb Conjugation, Tenses & Moods (Page 12)
### Chapter 5: Verb Conjugation, Tenses & Moods
CHAPTER 5: VERB CONJUGATION, TENSES & MOODS
Summary: Verbal roots, tense inflections (-ei, -ser, -ra), imperative (-lu), and negative prefixes.

TANGKHUL READING TEXT:
I sai (I do, present), i sada / i theiser (past), i sara (future).
Imperative: salu (do!), theilu (see!). Negative: masa-mana (does not
do), mathei-mana (does not see). Causative: -sak (sasaku).

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
Verbs conjugate through tense suffixes: present -ei/-i, past -ser/-da,
future -ra. Imperative forms take suffix -lu. Negation frames the verbal
root with prefix ma- and suffix -mana. Causation is expressed with -sak.

## Chapter 6: Specimen I: The Parable of the Prodigal Son (Complete) (Page 14)
### Chapter 6: Specimen I: The Parable of the Prodigal Son (Complete)
CHAPTER 6: SPECIMEN I: THE PARABLE OF THE PRODIGAL SON (COMPLETE)
Summary: Authentic full-text translation of Luke 15:11-32 in Tangkhul Naga with verse-by-verse gloss.

TANGKHUL READING TEXT:
11. Mi akhali mayarnaowon khani leiyi. 12. Kakhani chiwui ngachaili
kakhanao china aishavaali kahang, 'Ava, ramwui kashikhavai ngashan iwui
khavai thali ili milu.' Chiwui vang aishavana aram athum khaniwui
ngachaili ngashanserie. 13. Zingkum kachung mathaloikhalang kakhanao
china aishapup sanglaga kashak ramli vashitserie, chitharan aphasa
kakhano awui vang phasa ngalung khayai eina khamatai thotna. 14. Ana
atam sashidaran ram chithuili haora kapha thotna; chitharan a kacham
thotna. 15. Chiwui vang a vashitlaga chiwui ramwui mi akhali phungphada
leiyi; ana ali awui lili hok kashukhavai ngasikseriya. 16. Chitharan ana
hokna shakhavai theichon chieina phasa vashikhavai ningkachon leiyi;
chalieikha mi akhana ali mimana. 17. Kha ana awui ning kazil chitharan
ana kahang, 'Iva wui ngavaia mi kachungkhana kachak mathaloikhalang
thari, kha i hiwui phasa ngalung kazata eina thiyei!' 18. 'I ngakhokda
iva wui lili vara, ana ali hangra: Ava, Kazingramli kala na
makhaingalina i yor kasa mi thotna.' 19. 'Chiwui vang nawui nao kachi
tui shiyan katha mavana; ili nawui ngavaia mi akha gaka thada sanglu.'
20. Chithālaga a ngakhokda aishava wui lili vayi. Kha a kashakli
leitharan aishavana ali theilaga kachan pamei, chithālaga ngathimlaga
akukli chukda kachamseriya. 21. Nao china ali kahang, 'Ava, Kazingramli
kala na makhaingalina i yor kasa mi thotna; nawui nao kachi tui shiyan
katha mavana.' 22. Kha aishavana awui ngavaia mi bingli kahang,
'Kaphāmei kachon sanglalu, ali khonlu; kala awui pangyali chap sanglu,
awui pheili khokhop sanglu.' 23. 'Kala se phapharo shithalu, ngasotlu;
ithum sashilaga mathaseru.' 24. 'Iwui nao hi thida leikha kazing thotna,
themda leikha theikhuiraser.' Chitharan athum mathapameiser. 25.
Chitharan awui mayarnaowon kakhara a lili leiyi; a ngateilaga shim
mathatungda leikhara laa khavai kala ngakhum kaphei khangazip tharan a
kashana. 26. Ana ngavaia mi akhali hoklaga, 'Hi khi thotno?' da
ngahangyei. 27. Ana ali kahang, 'Nawui nanao vashungser, nawui avana se
phapharo shithiser, ana phasa katha theikhuira tharan.' 28. Chiwui vang
a kachuk thotna, a shimli ungkhavai ningkachon maleimana; chiwui vang
aishava vashitlaga ali kahangyei. 29. Ana ngashitda aishavaali kahang,
'Theilu, kum hiya thada i nali ngavaiyei, nawui tui kachikha mamatai-
mana; chithakha iwui azeishinao bingli ngasotda sashikhavai senao akha
gaka ili mimana.' 30. 'Kha nawui nao khamatai thotna ngalanao bingli
nawui ram shiser china vang se phapharo shithimina!' 31. Ana ali kahang,
'Nao, na i eina kapam thotna, iwui ot phasa shishapup nawuina.' 32. 'Kha
mathada ngasotkhavai shiyan phamei; nanao hi thida leikha kazing thotna,
themda leikha theikhuiraser.'

ENGLISH TRANSLATION:
11. A certain man had two sons: 12. And the younger of them said to his
father, 'Father, give me the portion of goods that falleth to me.' And
he divided unto them his living. 13. And not many days after the younger
son gathered all together, and took his journey into a far country, and
there wasted his substance with riotous living. 14. And when he had
spent all, there arose a mighty famine in that land; and he began to be
in want. 15. And he went and joined himself to a citizen of that
country; and he sent him into his fields to feed swine. 16. And he would
fain have filled his belly with the husks that the swine did eat: and no
man gave unto him. 17. And when he came to himself, he said, 'How many
hired servants of my father's have bread enough and to spare, and I
perish with hunger!' 18. 'I will arise and go to my father, and will say
unto him, Father, I have sinned against heaven, and before thee,' 19.
'And am no more worthy to be called thy son: make me as one of thy hired
servants.' 20. And he arose, and came to his father. But when he was yet
a great way off, his father saw him, and had compassion, and ran, and
fell on his neck, and kissed him. 21. And the son said unto him,
'Father, I have sinned against heaven, and in thy sight, and am no more
worthy to be called thy son.' 22. But the father said to his servants,
'Bring forth the best robe, and put it on him; and put a ring on his
hand, and shoes on his feet:' 23. 'And bring hither the fatted calf, and
kill it; and let us eat, and be merry:' 24. 'For this my son was dead,
and is alive again; he was lost, and is found.' And they began to be
merry. 25. Now his elder son was in the field: and as he came and drew
nigh to the house, he heard music and dancing. 26. And he called one of
the servants, and asked what these things meant. 27. And he said unto
him, 'Thy brother is come; and thy father hath killed the fatted calf,
because he hath received him safe and sound.' 28. And he was angry, and
would not go in: therefore came his father out, and intreated him. 29.
And he answering said to his father, 'Lo, these many years do I serve
thee, neither transgressed I at any time thy commandment: and yet thou
never gavest me a kid, that I might make merry with my friends:' 30.
'But as soon as this thy son was come, which hath devoured thy living
with harlots, thou hast killed for him the fatted calf.' 31. And he said
unto him, 'Son, thou art ever with me, and all that I have is thine.'
32. 'It was meet that we should make merry, and be glad: for this thy
brother was dead, and is alive again; and was lost, and is found.'
