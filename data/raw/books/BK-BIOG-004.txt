PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Rowland Suisa was one of the most original political thinkers of
Northeast India. Born in Somdal village, he served as Member of
Parliament and dedicated his life to bridging differences between the
Government of India and Naga national aspirations through constitutional
federation and peaceful coexistence.

CHAPTER 1: EARLY LIFE IN SOMDAL & EDUCATIONAL LEADERSHIP
Summary: Youth in Somdal, education in Jorhat and Shillong, and pioneer community organiz...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Rowland Suisa 1907-li Somdal village, Ukhrul district-li
pharisaye. Ana Jorhat Christian high school kala Shillongli education
khuiye. Somdal-li unglaga ana Tangkhul youth, teachers, kala
farmersbingli organize saye. Ana tribal customary law kala modern
constitutional democracy-wui ngachaili philosophical harmony theitserie.
English Translation / Meaning:
Rowland Suisa was born in 1907 in Somdal village, Ukhrul district. He
studied at Jorhat Christian High School and in Shillong. Returning to
Somdal, he organized teachers, youths, and farmers, discovering a deep
philosophical harmony between tribal customary law and modern
constitutional democracy.

CHAPTER 2: PARLIAMENTARY CAREER & THE FAMOUS 'SUISA'S PROPOSAL'
Summary: Election to the 2nd Lok Sabha (1957) and the formulating of Suisa's historic pea...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: 1957-li R. Suisa Outer Manipur constituency eina 2nd Lok
Sabha-li MP elect saye. Parliament-li ana hill tribeswui rights,
autonomy, kala economic justice-wui vang speak saye. Ana historical
'Suisa's Proposal' formulate saye: India kala Nagaswui ngachaili federal
association eina honorable peaceful co-existence kasakakhui. Nehru kala
Jayaprakash Narayan-na awui vision-li high respect miye.
English Translation / Meaning:
In 1957, R. Suisa was elected Member of Parliament to the 2nd Lok Sabha.
In Parliament, he passionately voiced the rights, autonomy, and
aspirations of hill tribal peoples. He drafted the historic 'Suisa's
Proposal', outlining an honorable federal association ensuring peaceful
co-existence. National leaders including Prime Minister Nehru and
Jayaprakash Narayan deeply respected his visionary statesmanship.

CHAPTER 3: LEGACY OF PEACEFUL DIALOGUE AND DEMOCRATIC PHILOSOPHY
Summary: Final years, passing in 1971, and enduring moral inspiration for peace in the Na...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: R. Suisana April 17, 1971 zingsho eina shok-unglakna. Awui
life and writingsna chitheiye: True peace violence eina rara mashuk-
mana; it must be built upon mutual respect, justice, and democratic
federation. Awui thoughts modern Naga peace process-li guiding light
thada leisaye.
English Translation / Meaning:
R. Suisa passed away on April 17, 1971. His life and philosophical
writings proved that true peace cannot be achieved through violence, but
must be constructed on mutual respect, justice, and democratic
federation. His ideas continue to serve as a beacon for the ongoing
peace process in the region.
