PREFACE (PETTIGREW, 1905)
The third Gospel presents in clear narrative form the life, parables, and healing ministries of our Lord. Care has been taken to preserve vernacular Tangkhul idioms of Ukhrul village, including agricultural and pastoral terms familiar to the hill tribes of Manipur. William Pettigrew, American Baptist Mission, Ukhrul, 1905.

BACK MATTER: LINGUISTIC AND SOCIOLOGICAL NOTES
Key Tangkhul terminology used in Luke (1905):
- Shiman-kahai: Lost, strayed, spiritually wandering.
- Ning-kongashi: Compassion, tender mercy, heartfelt empathy.
- Pangkaso: Laborer, hired worker, tenant cultivator.
- Mathan-kashei: Praise, reverence, holy exaltation.
