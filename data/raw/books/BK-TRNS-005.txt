PREFACE & TRANSLATION HISTORICAL CONTEXT
The translation of the Psalter into Tangkhul Naga represents a sublime
convergence between ancient Hebrew poetry and indigenous Tangkhul poetic
forms. The translators incorporated parallelisms, vivid mountain
metaphors, and pastoral idioms that resonate deeply with the highland
soul of the Tangkhul people.
Archival Catalog Reference: BSI-TKH-PSA-1968

CHAPTER 1: PSALM 1: THE TWO WAYS -- TREE BY THE WATER BROOKS
Summary: Contrast between the righteous person flourishing like a river tree and the chaf...
Tangkhul Text:
Psalm 1:1-4: Kaphā-kashana mikumo kachikhana kashikashibingwui
ningkazangli mashurla, yorshibingwui shongfali mangapamla, kala
khanganoibingwui pamkaphunli mangapam-mana; Kha ana Prohowui ngashan-
kasakazingli mathat-eina leikashiye, kala Awui tui zingsho ngazing
kachikhali thimkazape. Ana kongkha kaphā wui tui-ngapomli thingphun
phunkahai thada sakhura, ana awui thotli thei kaphā phāser-ra, awui
naorap mangapaorira; kala ana sakahai ot pupupli mashungsere.
Kashikashibing chi hithada maleimana: kha huina shuk-kakhui pakhara
thada kasaye.
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor
standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful.
But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he
meditate day and night. And he shall be like a tree planted by the
rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf
also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper. The
ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.

CHAPTER 1 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * kongkha (noun): river bank, mountain stream brook
    Ex: Kongkha kaphā wui tui.
  * naorap (noun): foliage, green leaves, arbor crown
    Ex: Awui naorap mangapaorira.
  * pakhara (noun): threshing chaff, husks blown by breeze
    Ex: Huina shuk-kakhui pakhara.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:

  - Simile with botanical imagery: 'thingphun phunkahai thada' (like a tree planted).

CHAPTER 2: PSALM 23: THE SHEPHERD PSALM (PROHO I-WUI NGASHANKASHAPANA)
Summary: The beloved pastoral psalm of comfort, protection, and overflowing blessing in l...
Tangkhul Text:
Psalm 23:1-6: Proho i-wui Ngashankashapana; ili khikha macham-mana. Ana
ili ngashalung kaphāli pamkazipsake, kala kongkhamathān tui-ngapomli ili
zaipinge. Ana iwui mangazingli mathangathuksake: Awui mingwui vang
mashung-kasa shongfali ili zaisange. I kathi ngachingwui longkhorli
zakhalangli, I kashikashili mangachek-mana; kasakei Na i eina li-leie;
Nawui thingchot kala deikhotna ili ngasot-e. Nawui mik-ngalemli
i-kashibingwui ngalemli maihon akha sakahaye; Nawui eina iwui kakhutli
meirong theiye; iwui kom kashunglaga tuksange. Mashungna iwui ringkapha
tharili kaphā kala ngashan china ili shursara: kala i Prohowui shimli
thot khamara kuppup pamkapamsara.
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. He maketh me to lie down in
green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters. He restoreeth my
soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name's sake.
Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will
fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort
me. Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies:
thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over. Surely goodness
and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in
the house of the LORD for ever.

CHAPTER 3: PSALM 91: THE SECRET PLACE OF THE MOST HIGH
Summary: Divine shelter under heavenly wings and deliverance from snare and pestilence....
Tangkhul Text:
Psalm 91:1-5, 11-12: Kaphāmei Kazingwui pamkaphunli pamkakhui chinana
Pangashan Kapeiwui ngaching tharili pamsara. I Prohowui vang hangsara:
'A iwui rungkahai pam, iwui lungkhor kashang, iwui Varivara; Ali I
chotkhangashit khuisara.' Ana vanaokhamakhui khangashun eina kala kasham
kashikaza eina nali huikhuira. Ana Awui theirei eina nali kuplupsara,
kala Awui phana ngashingli na rungpamsara: Awui mashung-tui nawui dhal
kala kot thada kasaye.
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under
the shadow of the Almighty. I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and
my fortress: my God; in him will I trust. Surely he shall deliver thee
from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence. He shall
cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his
truth shall be thy shield and buckler.

CHAPTER 3 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * pamkaphun (noun): secret sanctuary, hidden pavilion
    Ex: Kazingwui pamkaphun.
  * rungkahai pam (noun): refuge fortress, strong citadel
    Ex: Iwui rungkahai pam.
  * phana (noun): protective wing, outstretched pinions
    Ex: Awui phana ngashingli.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Defensive weaponry metaphors in Tangkhul: 'dhal' (shield) and 'kot' (buckler/armor).
  - Metaphorical shelter using avian wing terminology: 'phana'.

CHAPTER 4: PSALM 121: THE TRAVELER'S MOUNTAIN PSALM (KAPHUNGLI YANGLU)
Summary: Lifting eyes to the hills from whence cometh help; the keeper who never slumbers...
Tangkhul Text:
Psalm 121:1-8: I iwui mik kaphung kashangli thuk-kashok-e: iwui
khangachon kachikhali shokhungra? Iwui khangachon kazing kala ngalei
sakahai Prohowui eina shokhunge! Ana nawui kakhutli ngasotkazip
makasara: nali ngashankashapana mangaphama-mana! Theilu, Israel-li
ngashankashapana mangaphama-mana, mapila-mana. Prohona nawui
ngashankashapana; Prohona nawui kashikli nawui ngaching katha leisaye.
Zingsho eina zingkhamathānna nali makara, ngazing eina kachanna nali
mamakhura. Prohona nali kashikashili huikhuira: Ana nawui mangazingli
ngashansara.
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help. My
help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth. He will not
suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber.
Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep. The LORD
is thy keeper: the LORD is thy shade upon thy right hand. The sun shall
not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night. The LORD shall preserve
thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul.

--- Page 14 ---
CHAPTER 4 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * khangachon (noun): help, assistance, celestial deliverance
    Ex: Iwui khangachon.
  * mapila-mana (verb): never sleeps, watchful vigilance
    Ex: Mangaphama-mana, mapila-mana.
  * kachan (noun): moon, lunar luminary
    Ex: Ngazing eina kachanna.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Directional elevation verb: 'thuk-kashok-e' (to lift eyes upward toward peaks).
  - Paired physiological verbs of resting: 'mangaphama-mana, mapila-mana' (slumber not, sleep not).

CHAPTER 5: PSALM 150: THE GRAND DOXOLOGY WITH TRADITIONAL INSTRUMENTS
Summary: Praise the Lord with trumpet, harp, strings, dance, and clashing cymbals....
Tangkhul Text:
Psalm 150:1-6: Proholi mathat-eina kashaklu! Awui kathara shillongli
Varivarali mathatlu! Awui pangashankazingli Ali mathatlu! Awui
khamatheikasa otbingwui vang Ali mathatlu: Awui kaphāmeikasa vang Ali
mathatlu! Torot (trumpet) kakhong eina Ali mathatlu: Harp kala Luishat-
thing (lyre) eina Ali mathatlu! Tingteila (tambourine) kala pheichak
(dance) eina Ali mathatlu: Tui-thingkhanghui (stringed organs) kala Pung
(pipes) eina Ali mathatlu! Meikakhong chot eina Ali mathatlu: Khongyui
kapei khong eina Ali mathatlu! Ringkashap kapeina Proholi mathat-eina
kashaklu! Hallelujah!
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
Praise ye the LORD. Praise God in his sanctuary: praise him in the
firmament of his power. Praise him for his mighty acts: praise him
according to his excellent greatness. Praise him with the sound of the
trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp. Praise him with the
timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and organs.
Praise him upon the loud cymbals: praise him upon the high sounding
cymbals. Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. Praise ye the
LORD.

CHAPTER 5 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * pheichak (noun): dance, sacred choral movement
    Ex: Tingteila kala pheichak.
  * Pung (noun): bamboo pipe, flutes, musical wind organ
    Ex: Pung eina mathatlu.
  * Luishat-thing (noun): wooden harp, lyre stringed box
    Ex: Harp kala Luishat-thing.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Tangkhul traditional organology: 'pheichak' (dance), 'Pung' (pipes), 'tingteila' (traditional tambourine).
  - Repeated cohortative imperative: 'Ali mathatlu!' (Praise Him!).
