PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Major Ralengnao 'Bob' Khathing is celebrated as one of modern India's
most extraordinary nation-builders. Born in Ukhrul, Manipur, on February
28, 1912, he became the first person from the hill tribes of Manipur to
graduate from university (Cotton College, Guwahati). In World War II, he
fought valiantly against the Japanese in Burma with V Force, winning the
Military Cross. In February 1951, leading an Assam Rifles expedition
through treacherous Himalayan snowfields, he peacefully established
Indian civil administration over Tawang without firing a single shot. He
later helped build the Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), served as Chief
Secretary of Nagaland, and was appointed India's Ambassador to Burma.
Archival Catalog Reference: TSA-TKH-BIOG-KHATHING

CHAPTER 1: EARLY LIFE, EDUCATION IN UKHRUL & COTTON COLLEGE (1912-1939)
Summary: Birth in Ukhrul, Christian mission schooling, Cotton College graduation, and ear...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Ralengnao Bob Khathing February 28, 1912 zingsho eina Ukhrul
ramli pharisaye. Awui ava Rubichon kala awui ava-va Tangkhul Christian
community-wui respected leader-leisai. Ana Ukhrul mission school kala
Shillongli lairik kaphāmei hanglaga, Cotton College Guwahati eina B.A.
pass saye; a Manipur hill tribeswui ngachaili graduate khare mayarnao-
sai. Ana Ukhrul high school-li headmaster salaga Tangkhul yaronaobingli
education milaga nation-building-wui lambi chitheiye.
English Translation / Meaning:
Ralengnao Bob Khathing was born in Ukhrul, Manipur, on February 28,
1912. His parents were pioneer leaders of the early Tangkhul Christian
church and education movement. After brilliant schooling in Ukhrul and
Shillong, he graduated with a B.A. from Cotton College, Guwahati,
becoming the very first university graduate among the hill tribes of
Manipur. He returned to Ukhrul to serve as headmaster, inspiring
generations of Naga youths toward educational excellence.

CHAPTER 2: WORLD WAR II HEROISM & THE MILITARY CROSS (1939-1945)
Summary: Commissioned into the British Indian Army, commanding V Force guerillas behind J...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: World War II phoklakha, Bob Khathingna British Indian Army-li
commission khuiye. Ana 19th Hyderabad Regiment kala V Force guerilla
unit command saye. Manipur kala Burma frontier-li Japanese forces-wui
ngalemli ana heroic intelligence kala combat operations lead saye. Awui
gallantry kala courage theilaga British Crown-na ali Military Cross (MC)
kala MBE decoration miserie. A Tangkhul warrior pride-wui supreme
symbol-sai.
English Translation / Meaning:
When World War II erupted, Bob Khathing was commissioned into the
British Indian Army. He commanded the 19th Hyderabad Regiment and the
elite V Force guerrilla unit behind Japanese lines. Operating across the
rugged jungles of Manipur and the Burma frontier, he executed daring
reconnaissance and combat operations. For extraordinary gallantry under
fire, he was awarded the coveted Military Cross (MC) and the MBE,
becoming a supreme symbol of Tangkhul martial valour.

CHAPTER 3: THE HISTORIC TAWANG EXPEDITION OF FEBRUARY 1951
Summary: Leading 200 Assam Rifles troops across snow-bound Sela Pass to establish Indian ...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: February 1951-li Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru kala Sardar
Patel-wui directive eina Major Bob Khathingna 200 Assam Rifles jawans
lead saye. Athumna snow-covered Sela Pass (13,700 feet) cross salaga
Tawangli vashukrie. Chithuili ana Monpa elders, Buddhist Monks kala
Tawang Monastery Abbot eina diplomatic talks saye. Khikha bullet
mashukla, ana Indian national flag Tawangli phungsangserie kala civil
administration phokserie! Hi Indian geopolitical history-li greatest
peaceful expedition hoda reot-kasaye.
English Translation / Meaning:
In February 1951, under strategic directives from New Delhi, Major Bob
Khathing led an expedition of 200 Assam Rifles troops. Braving
blizzards, they crossed the treacherous Sela Pass at 13,700 feet and
entered Tawang. With consummate diplomatic tact, Khathing held parleys
with Monpa village chieftains, head lamas, and the Abbot of Tawang
Monastery. Without firing a single shot, he raised the Indian tricolour
over Tawang and established civil administration, securing India's
strategic northern border in one of the greatest peaceful military
expeditions in modern history.

CHAPTER 4: AMBASSADOR TO BURMA, STATESMANSHIP & PADMA SHRI (1951-1990)
Summary: Founding the SSB, serving as Chief Secretary of Nagaland, Ambassador to Myanmar,...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 4: Tawangwui tharan, Bob Khathingna Special Frontier Force kala
SSB-li founding leader salaga ot sai. Ana Nagaland State-wui Chief
Secretary salaga peace kala administrative stability phung-ungmi.
1972-li Government of India-na ali Ambassador to Burma (Myanmar) appoint
saye; a first tribal diplomat from Northeast India hoda history
sakahaye. President of India-na ali Padma Shri honor miserie. January
12, 1990 zingsho eina ana Mantripukhri Imphal-li shok-unglakna; awui
ming Tangkhul kala India-wui history-li thot pupup ngashan-leisara!
English Translation / Meaning:
Following Tawang, Bob Khathing served as a foundational architect of the
Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB). He served as Chief Secretary of Nagaland,
ushering in peace and administrative modernization during turbulent
times. In 1972, the Government of India appointed him Ambassador to
Burma, making him the first tribal diplomat from Northeast India to head
a diplomatic mission abroad. He was conferred the national honor of
Padma Shri. He passed away on January 12, 1990, in Imphal, leaving an
immortal legacy of patriotism, courage, and integrity.
