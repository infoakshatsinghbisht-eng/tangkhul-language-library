PREFACE BY THE TRANSLATOR
This version of the Fourth Gospel in the Tangkhul Naga language is the first continuous scripture portion issued for the people inhabiting the hills of Eastern Manipur. The orthography follows the system adopted in the Tangkhul Primer (1897), employing Roman characters with diacritical marks to represent open vowel sounds. Ukhrul, 1904. — William Pettigrew.

BACK MATTER & VOCABULARY NOTES
Linguistic glossary of theological adaptations introduced in the 1904 Gospel of St. John:
- Varivara: Supreme Creator Deity (adapted for God Almighty).
- Tui: Word, speech, discourse (used for Logos / Greek Divine Word).
- Mangan / Mangan Kathara: Holy Spirit, spiritual life-force.
- Haring: Life, spiritual living, salvation.
- Tuikhor: Well of living water, fountainhead.
