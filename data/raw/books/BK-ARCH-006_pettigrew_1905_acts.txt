PREFACE (ACTS IN TANGKHUL NAGA, 1905)
The Book of the Acts illustrates the spread of the Christian church from Jerusalem through Antioch to the Greco-Roman world. In this Tangkhul translation, the term 'Kachatbing' is used for Apostles (those sent forth with authority). Early Tangkhul converts in Ukhrul assisted in verifying village speech idioms. — W. Pettigrew.

BACK MATTER: LINGUISTIC ADAPTATIONS & GLOSSARY
Vocabulary innovations in Pettigrew's Acts (1905):
- Kachatbing: Apostles (messengers authorized to speak for Christ).
- Theishak: Casting lots / traditional determination of divine will.
- Areopagus / Hangva-pam: Council hill / tribunal of elders.
- Mathikahai Varivara: Unknown God (deity beyond formal names).
