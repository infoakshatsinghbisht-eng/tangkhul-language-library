PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Wungram Okthot weaves ethnohistorical authenticity with powerful
novelistic storytelling. It preserves traditional terminology for
ancient rituals, clan feasts of merit (Maron), wood-carving on village
gates (Kharashim), and the philosophical foundations of Tangkhul
identity.

CHAPTER 1: THE FEAST OF MERIT (MARON KASA)
Summary: Depicts an elder staging the grand feast of merit, erecting sacrificial monolith...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Wungram elder akhana Maron (Feast of Merit) sakhavai
ningkashang leisai. Maron kasana village-li saphā-kashang khangam
machitheila, awui lungkashung eina mikumo pupupli kachak kala khor
milaga village unity ngashankhavai leisai. Ana cattle thakharā
shaksarlaga shang pupupli ngashanserie. Kala awui shim-ngalemli
lungphung monolith akha kakor-hamsangie.
English Translation / Meaning:
An elder of Wungram resolved to perform the solemn Feast of Merit
(Maron). This feast was intended not for vain ostentation, but to
generously distribute cattle meat and rice brew to every clan, thereby
cementing the spiritual and social unity of the village. He slaughtered
cattle and distributed portions to all clans, and erected a memorial
monolith beside his homestead.

CHAPTER 2: THE CARVING OF THE VILLAGE GATE (KHARASHIM TARUNG)
Summary: Master woodcarvers adorn the sacred village entrance gate with horned mithun emb...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: Village-wui gate (Kharashim) kathara sakhavai, thing-
artisanbingna Tarung kashang thingphun khuilaga village-li phung-
ungserie. Athumna mithun (siel) horn, kazing-sira, kala warriors-wui
emblem kaphāmei khak-saye. Kharashim china village-li kashikashili eina
huikhuikhavai sacred barrier thada kasaye.
English Translation / Meaning:
To renew the village gateway (Kharashim), master artisans carried the
sacred Tarung timber from the virgin forest. With adzes and chisels,
they carved motifs of mithun horns, celestial stars, and brave warriors.
The consecrated gate served as both a physical fortification and a
spiritual shield safeguarding the village from evil.

CHAPTER 3: THE COVENANT OF THE FOUR MOUNTAIN VILLAGES
Summary: Four historically warring mountain settlements establish a permanent peace pact ...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: Kaphung matiwui village-bingna ngaburlaga peace treaty
sakahaisai. Athumna haikaphā salaga: 'Ithui yaronaobingna warfare
kashikahailaga luiramli ot sara; ithui ngalanaobingna inter-marriage
eina shangli unity phungsara.' Wungram kaphung tharili peace-wui
mayāngwon shokserie.
English Translation / Meaning:
The four mountain villages assembled and consecrated a permanent peace
pact. They solemnly pledged: 'Our youth shall cast aside war and till
the terraced fields; our daughters shall unite the clans through
honorable intermarriage.' And over the heights of Wungram, the rainbow
of enduring peace shone bright.
