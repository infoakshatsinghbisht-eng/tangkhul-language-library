PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Rishang Keishing was a titan of Indian democracy and the pride of the
Tangkhul Naga community. Born in Bungpa village in 1920, he was elected
to India's first Lok Sabha in 1952, working alongside Jawaharlal Nehru.
He served as Chief Minister of Manipur across multiple tumultuous
decades, championing inter-community harmony, highland development, and
democratic constitutionalism.

CHAPTER 1: FROM BUNGPA VILLAGE TO INDIA'S FIRST LOK SABHA (1920-1952)
Summary: Youth in southern Tangkhul village Bungpa, St. Paul's College Calcutta, and elec...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Rishang Keishing October 25, 1920 zingsho eina Bungpa Khullen
village-li pharisaye. Ana Calcutta St. Paul's Cathedral Mission College
eina graduate saye. 1952-li Indiawui first General Election shoklakha, a
Outer Manipur Parliamentary constituency eina first Lok Sabha-li Member
of Parliament (MP) elect saye. A Parliament of India-wui youngest MPs
tharili akha leisai.
English Translation / Meaning:
Rishang Keishing was born on October 25, 1920, in Bungpa Khullen
village, Ukhrul district. He graduated from St. Paul's Cathedral Mission
College, Calcutta. In India's historic first general election in 1952,
he was elected to the first Lok Sabha from Outer Manipur, becoming one
of the youngest and most articulate parliamentarians in free India.

CHAPTER 2: CHIEF MINISTER OF MANIPUR & CHAMPION OF COMMUNAL HARMONY
Summary: Serving as Chief Minister during difficult decades, balancing hill-valley unity ...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: Rishang Keishingna multiple terms-wui vang Manipur State-wui
Chief Minister salaga ot sai. Ana valley kala hill tribeswui ngachaili
peace, secular unity, kala infrastructural development phung-ungmi. Ana
high schools, roads, water supply, kala hospitals kaphung-longkhor
kuppupli shimsakserie. Ana democratic dialogue eina conflict resolution-
li faith khuiye.
English Translation / Meaning:
Rishang Keishing served multiple tenures as Chief Minister of Manipur.
Navigating ethnic conflicts and insurgency, he steadfastly championed
harmony between the valley and hill communities. He expanded roads, high
schools, electrification, and medical centers across remote hill
districts, believing passionately in democratic constitutional dialogue.

CHAPTER 3: ELDER STATESMAN OF THE RAJYA SABHA & FINAL YEARS (1920-2017)
Summary: Elder statesman in the Rajya Sabha retiring at age 94, and his enduring legacy o...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: Rishang Keishingna 2014 thakli Rajya Sabha MP salaga public
service-li leisai, a Indiawui oldest sitting parliamentarian hoda retire
saye (age 94). August 22, 2017 zingsho eina ana Imphal-li shok-unglakna.
Awui 60 years public service Tangkhul people-wui democratic pride thada
history-li thot pupup mathā-leisara.
English Translation / Meaning:
Rishang Keishing continued serving in the Rajya Sabha until 2014,
retiring as India's oldest sitting parliamentarian at the age of 94. He
passed away on August 22, 2017, in Imphal. His six decades of
distinguished public service remain an enduring source of democratic
pride for the Tangkhul people and the nation.
