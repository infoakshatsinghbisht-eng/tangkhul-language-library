PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Rev. William Pettigrew is revered by the Tangkhul people as the
architect of their modern literature and schooling. Arriving in Manipur
in 1894 and establishing the Ukhrul mission station in 1896, he spent 35
years compiling the first Tangkhul grammar, dictionary, arithmetic
manuals, scripture translations, and hygienic primers.

CHAPTER 1: ARRIVAL IN UKHRUL & THE FIRST ROMAN ALPHABET (1894-1897)
Summary: Pettigrew's arrival in Manipur, invitation by Chief Raihao to Ukhrul, and reduci...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Rev. William Pettigrew 1869-li Edinburgh Scotland-li
pharisaye. 1894-li ana Manipur-li rarlaga, Political Agent Major
Maxwell-wui permission eina Ukhrul kaphungli vashukrie. Ukhrul headman
Awungshi Raihaona ali welcome saye. Chitharan Tangkhul bhashali written
alphabet maleisai. Pettigrewna Ukhrul dialect study salaga Roman script
adopt saye, kala 1897-li first Tangkhul Primer (Tuitam Lairik) publish
saye.
English Translation / Meaning:
Rev. William Pettigrew was born in Edinburgh, Scotland, in 1869.
Arriving in Manipur in 1894, he received permission from Political Agent
Major Maxwell to proceed to the Ukhrul hills in 1896. Chief Raihao of
Ukhrul welcomed him into the village. At that time, Tangkhul was an
unwritten oral language. Pettigrew mastered the Ukhrul dialect, adapted
the Roman alphabet, and published the historic first Tangkhul Primer in
1897, founding modern literacy.

CHAPTER 2: LEXICOGRAPHY, GRAMMAR & THE 1927 NEW TESTAMENT
Summary: Compiling the 1918 monumental Grammar & Dictionary, school textbooks, and the 19...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: Pettigrewna native scholarsbing eina collaboration salaga
1918-li 'Tangkhul Naga Grammar and Dictionary' Shillong Government Press
eina publish saye. Ana history, geography, hygiene, kala arithmetic-wui
textbooks kasasaye. Awui greatest achievement-na 1927-li British &
Foreign Bible Society eina 'Ithui Varewui Lairik Kathara' (Complete New
Testament) publish kasasai. Hi Tangkhul standard literary prose-wui
foundation thada kasaye.
English Translation / Meaning:
Collaborating closely with native Tangkhul elders, Pettigrew published
the seminal 'Tangkhul Naga Grammar and Dictionary' in 1918. He authored
textbooks in history, geography, arithmetic, and public health. His
crowning literary achievement was the 1927 publication of the complete
Tangkhul New Testament, which established the grammatical and stylistic
canon of standard modern Tangkhul prose.

CHAPTER 3: LEGACY & ENDURING IMPACT ON TANGKHUL SOCIETY
Summary: Retirement in 1933, death in 1943, and lasting cultural institutions established...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: 1933-li Pettigrewna retirement khuilaga Britain-li ung-
kaphungsaye. January 19, 1943-li a England-li shok-unglakna. Kha ana
Ukhrul-li seeds phunkahai education, medical dispensaries, kala
literature china thot pupup Tangkhul nation-li transform saye. Ukhrul-li
awui memorial library, colleges, kala monuments thot pupup Ali 'Father
of Tangkhul Literacy' hoda reot-kasa leisaye.
English Translation / Meaning:
In 1933, Pettigrew retired and returned to the United Kingdom, passing
away on January 19, 1943. Yet the seeds of literacy, public health, and
literature he planted in Ukhrul transformed the Tangkhul people into one
of the most literate and progressive communities in Northeast India.
Memorial libraries, educational institutions, and monuments throughout
Ukhrul honor him as the Father of Tangkhul Literacy.
