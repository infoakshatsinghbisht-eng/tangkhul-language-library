PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Shongza is a cornerstone text of Tangkhul higher education, combining
the narrative breadth of a novel with the tense dialogue of classical
Greek and Shakespearean tragedy. It probes the depths of 'ngashan'
(customary honour) and demonstrates the tragic human cost of unforgiving
generational vendettas.
Archival Catalog Reference: TLS-TKH-NOVEL-SHONGZA

CHAPTER 1: THE OATH OF VENGEANCE (KASHUNGWUI TUI-NGASHIK)
Summary: An ancient ancestral grievance sparks an oath between rival mountain chieftains....
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Shongzana awui avashimwui thàng khuilaga kaphung kashangli
yangsaye. Kum kachung ngaphalashu, awui avawui kathi chi awui lungli
meikatha thada kazeiye. Ana awui uningnaobingli hang: 'Shangwui phashan
kashiman-khalang, mikumona katha eina ringkapamsara? I tui-ngashik saye,
mashung-kasa raser-ra!' Kha awui ava-va kachamna hang: 'Shongza,
vashitsang-kasa eina vashitsang kacham rara; ngashankahaimilu!'
English Translation / Meaning:
Shongza unsheathed his father's ancestral dagger and gazed across the
mountain frontiers. Though years had passed, the memory of his father's
untimely demise burned like unquenchable fire in his breast. He told his
clan brothers: 'If our lineage honour is degraded, how can we walk with
head held high? I swear a sacred oath, justice shall be executed!' Yet
his wise mother cautioned him: 'O Shongza, vengeance begetteth only
destruction; grant forgiveness!'

CHAPTER 1 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Vocabulary Entries:
  * phashan (noun): lineage honour, clan prestige, dignity
    Ex: Shangwui phashan.
  * vashitsang (noun): retribution, vendetta, blood vengeance
    Ex: Vashitsang eina.
  * ngashankahaimilu (verb): forgive, grant clemency and pardon
    Ex: Ngashankahaimilu!
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Honorific clan speech: 'Shangwui phashan' (dignity of the patriline).
  - Hortative contrast: oath of revenge vs maternal plea for clemency.

CHAPTER 2: THE TRIAL BEFORE THE VILLAGE HANGVA (HANGVALI ADJUDICATION)
Summary: The village elders convene at the sacred megalith circle to arbitrate between th...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: Village Hangvawui elder-bingna lungphung kashang ngalemli
pamkazipserie. Awungshina ceremonial tui-khak honsaye: 'Hao ngashanli
mikumo kashikan khui mashuk-mana. Ithum tui-ngalung eina mashung-kasa
sakakhui leisaye.' Shongzana awui tuitam chitheiye, kha rival chieftain-
na restitution pay-sakhavai cattle kala woven Haora shawl offer saye.
Hangvana tui khaklaga tui-ngalung treaty seal saye.
English Translation / Meaning:
The elders of the Hangva gathered beside the ancestral megalithic stone
circle. The village chief pronounced the opening adjudication formula:
'Under customary law, no man may take the law into his own hands. We
must establish reconciliation through righteous restitution.' Shongza
presented his grievance, and the rival clan offered restitution of
cattle and ceremonial Haora shawls. The council rendered its verdict,
establishing a binding covenant of peace.

CHAPTER 3: THE RECONCILED DAWN (ZINGKHAMATHĀN KATHARA)
Summary: Shongza chooses reconciliation over bloodshed, breaking the cycle of vendettas f...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: Shongzana awui thàng sheath-li sheitsangserie. Ana
theitserie: Mashung-kasana kashamli maphuma-mana, kha ringkapha kaphāli
phumye. Khor ceremonial pot eina athum khani clan-bingna ngayei tui
honserie. Ukhrul kaphungli zingkhamathān shoklakha, village
yaronaobingna mathat-eina Hao Pheichak pheitserie. Shongzana hang:
'Ithuwui naorap thot pupup ngacham eina ringkapamsara!'
English Translation / Meaning:
Shongza sheathed his dagger into its scabbard. He realized: True
strength lies not in shedding blood, but in preserving life and future
generations. Drinking from the ceremonial rice-beer horn, the two clans
sealed their sacred alliance. As dawn broke over the peaks of Ukhrul,
the youth joined in the festive circle dance. Shongza proclaimed: 'Our
children and grandchildren shall abide in enduring peace!'

CHAPTER 3 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Key Vocabulary Entries:
  * sheitsangserie (verb): sheathed back into the scabbard
    Ex: Thàng sheath-li sheitsang.
  * naorap (noun): descendants, posterity, offspring
    Ex: Ithuwui naorap thot pupup.
  * ngacham eina (adv): peacefully, in harmony
    Ex: Ngacham eina ringkapam.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Climactic resolution: sheathing the weapon symbolizes transition from warrior vendetta to civic peace.
  - Blessing for posterity: 'naorap thot pupup'.
