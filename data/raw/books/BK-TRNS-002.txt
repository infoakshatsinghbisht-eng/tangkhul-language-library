PREFACE & TRANSLATION HISTORICAL CONTEXT
The Gospel of St. Matthew was translated into the Tangkhul Naga language
in Ukhrul between 1905 and 1907. Printed at the Baptist Mission Press,
Calcutta, under the auspices of the British and Foreign Bible Society.
It provides foundational examples of early Tangkhul theological
orthography, honorific expressions, and didactic prose.
Archival Catalog Reference: BFBS-TKH-MATT-1907

CHAPTER 1: THE GENEALOGY AND NATIVITY OF CHRIST (MATTHEW 1-2)
Summary: The generational lineage and the arrival of the Wise Men in Bethlehem rendered i...
Tangkhul Text:
Matthew 1:18-21: Jesu Christawui kaphari hithada leisai: Awui ava Maryli
Joseph eina phankahai ngachaoda maleiranglakha, ana Kazing Mangazing
Katharawui eina phasa kapang theitserie. Chitharan awui va Josephna
mikumo mashungna kahana, ali kakhana phasa maleiranglakha khangareili
zanglaga kashang-phā eina thang-khavai ning khami leisai. Kala ana
naomayār akha sashapra, kala nawui eina ali JESU hoda kahang sara,
kasakei Ana awui mikumoli athumwui yorshi eina huikhui sara.
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
Now the birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise: When as his mother Mary
was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with
child of the Holy Ghost. Then Joseph her husband, being a just man, and
not willing to make her a publick example, was minded to put her away
privily. And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name
JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.

CHAPTER 1 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * kaphari (noun): birth, nativity, genealogy
    Ex: Jesu Christawui kaphari.
  * phankahai (verb): betrothed, pledged in marriage
    Ex: Joseph eina phankahai.
  * yorshi (noun): sin, moral trespass, transgression
    Ex: Yorshi eina huikhui.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Postposition 'ngachaoda' indicates temporal precedence ('prior to', 'before').
  - Causative verbal suffix '-shuk' or future volitional '-sara' (huikhui sara = shall redeem/save).

CHAPTER 2: THE LORD'S PRAYER & THE SERMON ON THE MOUNT (MATTHEW 6)
Summary: The classic Lord's Prayer (Ithui Kaphungli Kasa Ava) and ethical teachings on pr...
Tangkhul Text:
Matthew 6:9-13: Chiwui vang nathumna hithada haikaphā salu: 'Ithui
kazingramli kaphung Ava, Nawui ming katharaseru. Nawui ngashan
kasakazing raseru. Nawui ningkazang kazingramli thada ngaleili
ngasokalu. Ithuli tharan kashakhavai kachak zingsho zingsho ithuli milu.
Kala ithumna ithuli kashikashili ngashankahaimi thada, ithuwui yorshili
ngashankahaimilu. Kala ithuli kakhayangli makaso-alu, kashikashili
huikhuilu; kasakei ngashankasakazing, kala pangashan, kala phashan
chinawui kuppup thotli nawuisai. Amen.'
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven,
Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it
is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread. And forgive us our
debts, as we forgive our debtors. And lead us not into temptation, but
deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the
glory, for ever. Amen.

CHAPTER 2 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * zingsho (noun): day, daily period
    Ex: Zingsho zingsho (day by day, daily).
  * ningkazang (noun): will, sovereign desire, intent
    Ex: Nawui ningkazang.
  * phashan (noun): glory, radiant honor, majestic praise
    Ex: Phashan nawuisai.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Direct imperative suffix '-lu' on verbal stems: salu (do/pray!), milu (give!), huikhuilu (deliver!).
  - Prohibitive negative imperative: 'ma-...-alu' in 'makaso-alu' (do not lead!).

CHAPTER 3: PARABLES OF THE KINGDOM OF HEAVEN (MATTHEW 13)
Summary: The Parable of the Sower and the Pearl of Great Price in rural agricultural Tang...
Tangkhul Text:
Matthew 13:3-8: 'Theilu, kachit kasana chitchikhavai vaye. Kala ana
chitkhalangli, charakha shongzali tuksangie, kala vanaona rarlaga
shaksangserie. Charakha ngalung thotli tuksangie, chithuili ngalei
kachung malei; kala ngalei maleiranglakha khangateili phayokaserie. Kha
zinga rar-daran chi meiratsangie, kala ngari maleimana chiwui vang
kangsangserie. Charakha suh thotli tuksangie, kala suhna shoklaga
pomsangserie. Kha charakha ngalei kaphāli tuksangie, kala theirarlaga
akha eina thakharā, akha eina tharuk-magā, akha eina magā thotna.'
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
Behold, a sower went forth to sow; And when he sowed, some seeds fell by
the way side, and the fowls came and devoured them up: Some fell upon
stony places, where they had not much earth: and forthwith they sprung
up, because they had no deepness of earth: And when the sun was up, they
were scorched; and because they had no root, they withered away. And
some fell among thorns; and the thorns sprung up, and choked them: But
other fell into good ground, and brought forth fruit, some an
hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.

  * shongza (noun): roadside, pathway margin

CHAPTER 4: THE GOLDEN RULE & THE NARROW GATE (MATTHEW 7)
Summary: Practical ethical guidance: judging others, seeking wisdom, and the narrow gate ...
Tangkhul Text:
Matthew 7:7-14: 'Hanglu, chitharan nathumli misara; khayorlu, chitharan
nathumna phāsara; shimkakhong kakorlu, chitharan nathumli hungkahang
misara. Kachikathāna hangkakhui thapup khuiye; kala khayorkakhui thapup
phāye; kala kakorkakhuili hungkahang miye. Khong kakatli zanglu; kasakei
khong khangashir kala shongfa kakhangashir china kashili vashukana, kala
zangkakhui bing kachungsai. Kha khong kakat kala shongfa kaphā china
ringkaphali vashukana, kala chichi phākakhui bing kachamlakna.'
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it
shall be opened unto you: For every one that asketh receiveth; and he
that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened. Enter
ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way,
that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat:
Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto
life, and few there be that find it.

--- Page 14 ---
CHAPTER 4 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * khayor (verb): to search, seek diligently
    Ex: Khayorlu, phāsara.
  * kakor (verb): to knock, rap on the gate
    Ex: Shimkakhong kakorlu.
  * shongfa (noun): path, gateway road
    Ex: Shongfa kakat.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Correlative paired clauses: 'hanglu... misara' (ask... it shall be given).
  - Antonyms in Tangkhul ethics: 'kasham/kashi' (destruction) vs 'ringkapha' (life).

CHAPTER 5: THE TRANSFIGURATION & HEALING (MATTHEW 17)
Summary: Christ's transfiguration on the high mountain and the healing of the afflicted y...
Tangkhul Text:
Matthew 17:1-5: Zing kum tharuk ngaphalaga, Jesuna Peter, James, kala
awui khanao Johnli kaphung kashang akhali athum kacham eina
thukpingsangie. Kala athum mik-ngalemli Awui aphasa khangatei thotna:
Awui mai zingkhamathān katha thada ngashangie, kala Awui kachon
mangashan katha thada kachak-ngayei thotna. Kala theilu, athumli Moses
kala Elijah tui-hanglakha theiserie.
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
And after six days Jesus taketh Peter, James, and John his brother, and
bringeth them up into an high mountain apart, And was transfigured
before them: and his face did shine as the sun, and his raiment was
white as the light. And, behold, there appeared unto them Moses and
Elias talking with him.

CHAPTER 5 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * kaphung (noun): mountain, highland peak
    Ex: Kaphung kashang.
  * kachon (noun): garment, woven robe, shawl
    Ex: Awui kachon mangashan.
  * kachak-ngayei (adj): pure white, radiantly gleaming
    Ex: Kachak-ngayei thotna.

  - Color and radiance descriptive compound: 'kachak-ngayei' (radiantly white).

CHAPTER 6: THE GREAT COMMISSION & EPILOGUE (MATTHEW 28)
Summary: The resurrection, triumph over death, and the universal apostolic commission....
Tangkhul Text:
Matthew 28:18-20: Kala Jesuna athum ngalemli rarlaga hangmi: 'Kazingram
kala ngaleili pangashan kuppup ili miserie. Chiwui vang nathumna valaga
mikumo ngashan pupup-li tuitam kasalu, Avawui, Naomayārwui, kala Kazing
Mangazing Katharawui ming eina athumli baptism milu. Kala I nathumli
sakasashikahai ot pupupli shurkhavai tuitam kasalu: kala theilu, I
thotwui kashikhavai thada zingsho pupup nathum eina li-leisara. Amen.'
English Translation / Hindi Meaning:
And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me
in heaven and in earth. Go ye therefore, and teach all nations,
baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the
Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have
commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the
world. Amen.

CHAPTER 6 -- VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR
Vocabulary Entries:
  * mikumo ngashan (noun): all nations, diverse tribes, humankind
    Ex: Ngashan pupup.
  * tuitam (noun/verb): teaching, instruction, discipleship
    Ex: Tuitam kasalu.
  * thotwui kashikhavai (noun): end of the age, consummation of time
    Ex: Thotwui kashikhavai thada.
Grammar Notes & Idiomatic Usage:
  - Universal quantifier 'pupup' attached to nominals (ngashan pupup = all nations; ot pupup = all commands).
  - Continuous presence promise marked by future auxiliary: 'li-leisara' (shall continuously abide).
