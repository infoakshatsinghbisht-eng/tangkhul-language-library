PREFACE & HISTORICAL CONTEXT
Luingam Luikham and his family represent the premier folklore collectors
and customary law codifiers of the Tangkhul nation. Their exhaustive
field collections preserved hundreds of ancient folk songs, clan
migration genealogies, and legal precedents before they could be eroded
by rapid modernization.
Archival Catalog Reference: TLS-TKH-BIOG-LUIKHAM

CHAPTER 1: THE QUEST FOR TANGKHUL ORAL HERITAGE
Summary: Field expeditions across remote villages collecting songs, riddles, clan genealo...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 1: Luingam Luikhamna Ukhrul, Kamjong, Somra, kala Phadangwui
remote villages-li field research eina zatpingsaye. Ana traditional
elders, folk singers, kala village court headmen eina interview saye.
Ana audio recordings, written notebooks, kala sacred oral songs kazip-
kasasaye. Ana theitserie: Hao culture oral tradition-li rich-lakna, chi
preserve kasa compulsory-sai.
English Translation / Meaning:
Luingam Luikham undertook pioneering field expeditions across remote
villages in Ukhrul, Kamjong, Somra, and Phadang. He interviewed elder
bards, traditional singers, and customary court headmen. He recorded
oral songs, transcribed clan genealogies, and documented customary court
cases, realizing that preserving oral folklore was vital for the
cultural survival of the Tangkhul people.

CHAPTER 2: CODIFYING CUSTOMARY JURISPRUDENCE & FOLKLORE VOLUMES
Summary: Publishing foundational books on Tangkhul customary law, folk tales, and traditi...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 2: Ana TLS eina publication-wui vang books kasasaye: Hao Ngashan
customary law, Tungtuktui (riddles), Hao-laa (folk songs), kala clan
histories. Awui booksna modern legal courts, schools, kala researchers-
li standard reference thada serve saye. Ana Tangkhul bhashali rich
literary prestige miserie.
English Translation / Meaning:
Collaborating with the Tangkhul Literature Society, he published
landmark works on customary law, folk riddles, traditional poetry, and
clan migrations. His publications became standard reference manuals for
customary courts, schools, and university researchers, bestowing immense
literary prestige upon the language.

CHAPTER 3: INSPIRATION FOR FUTURE GENERATIONS OF SCHOLARS
Summary: The enduring impact of his preservation work on contemporary Naga cultural consc...
Tangkhul Text:
Chapter 3: Luingam Luikhamwui work-na modern Tangkhul youth-li cultural
pride kala identity-wui understanding miserie. Ukhrul-li ana setup kahai
archives modern linguists kala researchers-li guide saye. Awui name
Tangkhul literary history-li immortal guardian thada ngashan-leisara.
English Translation / Meaning:
Luingam Luikham's work instilled deep cultural pride and self-
understanding in younger generations. The archives and documentation he
established in Ukhrul continue to guide linguists and anthropological
researchers worldwide. His name will forever be remembered as an
immortal guardian of Tangkhul heritage.
