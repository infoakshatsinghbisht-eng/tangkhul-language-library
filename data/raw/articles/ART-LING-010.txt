TITLE: Grammaticalization of Motion Verbs into Directional Postpositions in Tangkhul
AUTHORS: Dr. Rekha Sharma, Dr. S. Arokianathan
JOURNAL / VENUE: Linguistics of the Tibeto-Burman Area (2008)
SUBJECT: Syntax, Semantics & Grammaticalization
KEYWORDS: Grammaticalization, Motion Verbs, Directionals, Deixis, Spatial Grammar, LTBA

ABSTRACT:
This paper investigates the grammaticalization pathways through which full lexical motion verbs in Tangkhul have developed into directional markers, telic aspectual affixes, and spatial postpositions. Drawing on connected texts spanning a century of documentation, we demonstrate that motion verbs specifying vertical axis (ascent/descent) and deictic orientation (centrifugal vs centripetal) systematically fuse with verbal stems to form complex path predicates.

SECTION: 1. Lexical Source Verbs & Deictic Trajectories
Tangkhul distinguishes three basic deictic path vectors: (i) Centripetal movement toward speaker (-ung eina ungkaphung), (ii) Centrifugal movement away from speaker (-va eina vazatkasa), and (iii) Altitude-conditioned vectors: ascent uphill (-ka) and descent downhill (-tuk).
Linguistic Data:
  * ung-kaphung : to arrive coming home toward speaker
    Notes: Centripetal motion.
  * va-zatkasa : to go away departing from speaker
    Notes: Centrifugal motion.
  * thuk-kashok : to climb up out of a gorge onto peak
    Notes: Ascending vertical vector.

SECTION: 2. Verbal Serialisation & Affixation
In serial verb constructions, the directional verb loses its independent nominal arguments and undergoes phonological reduction into an aspectual clitic. For instance, in 'a ot sa-va-ye' (He went off to do work), the auxiliary -va marks departure aspect rather than physical walking.
Linguistic Data:
  * sa-va-ye : did and departed / went off doing
    Notes: Grammaticalized departure clitic.
  * thei-shok-e : perceived clearly outward / manifested
    Notes: Directional verb shok (exit) grammaticalized as telic resultative.

SECTION: 3. Typological Parallels in Tibeto-Burman
The Tangkhul grammaticalization cline [Motion Verb -> Serial Verb -> Directional Affix -> Aspect Marker] directly mirrors paradigms observed in Qiang, Jingpho, and Lahu, providing evidence for areal Tibeto-Burman syntactic evolution.
Linguistic Data:
  * kashok (exit) -> -shok (telic completive) : Grammaticalization pathway
    Notes: Lexical verb to grammatical morpheme.
