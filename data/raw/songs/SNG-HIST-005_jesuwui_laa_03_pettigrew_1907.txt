[STANZA 1]
TANGKHUL:
Jesuwui vang nganinglu,
Krushwui raimi nashi;
Nawui philan khang khaninglu,
Mashimanpaimana.
Varina thanta laili,
Yenggashi mayuimara;
Ana pingta chiaina,
Apuk apag awung sai.

ENGLISH:
Stand up, stand up for Jesus, ye soldiers of the cross;
Lift high His royal banner, it must not suffer loss.
From victory unto victory His army shall He lead,
Till every foe is vanquished, and Christ is Lord indeed.

[STANZA 2]
TANGKHUL:
Jesuwui vang nganinglu,
Talla khon nganalu,
Nashina mangacheeaka
Aja raipam chatlu;
Yenggashi kachungkali,
Ningkachang aina yuilu;
Chakchinlaka themta salu,
Arui mangashong chatlu.

ENGLISH:
Stand up, stand up for Jesus, the trumpet call obey;
Forth to the mighty conflict in this His glorious day.
Ye that are men now serve Him against unnumbered foes;
Let courage rise with danger, and strength to strength oppose.

[STANZA 3]
TANGKHUL:
Jesuwui vang nganinglu,
Khangarar masangmara;
Aja gung gung kang gang sali,
Akhama la sara.
Kapinga mi chili
Kamata-va mira;
Varivarali ngasoda
Mashimanta pamra.

ENGLISH:
Stand up, stand up for Jesus, the strife will not be long;
This day the noise of battle, the next the victor's song.
To him that overcometh, a crown of life shall be;
He with the King of Glory shall reign eternally.
