[STANZA 1]
TANGKHUL:
Ili Jesuna laishi,
Khikala jila, ina
Vare chiwui lairikli,
Palaga I shitsanga.
Ma, ili laishi,
Lairikli iya.

ENGLISH:
Jesus loves me, this I know,
For the Bible tells me so;
Little ones to Him belong,
They are weak, but He is strong.
Yes, Jesus loves me,
The Bible tells me so.

[STANZA 2]
TANGKHUL:
Ili Jesuna laishi,
A mangla chihomiha-i;
Varina kaching khamong,
Shomida changmilakra.

ENGLISH:
Jesus loves me, He who died,
Heaven's gate to open wide;
He will wash away my sin,
Let His little child come in.

[STANZA 3]
TANGKHUL:
Ili Jesuna laishi,
Noshino awui nona;
Athum masathukmana,
Ana kamakangvana.

ENGLISH:
Jesus loves me, loves me still,
Though I'm very weak and ill;
From His shining throne on high,
Comes to watch me where I lie.

[STANZA 4]
TANGKHUL:
Hi Jesuna laishi,
Iwui moral pheomira;
Khikala jila awui
Mangla chihomihaira.

ENGLISH:
Jesus loves me, He will stay,
Close beside me all the way;
Thou hast bled and died for me,
I will henceforth live for Thee.

[STANZA 5]
TANGKHUL:
Hi Jesuna laishi,
Thikahai chitharanli,
Amaili shitsangaka
Kachingli changniilakra.

ENGLISH:
Jesus loves me, when I die,
He will take me home on high;
Trusting in His precious grace,
Dwelling in that heavenly place.
