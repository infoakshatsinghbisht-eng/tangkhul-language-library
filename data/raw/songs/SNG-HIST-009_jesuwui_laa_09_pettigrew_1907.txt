[STANZA 1]
TANGKHUL:
Arui chimik tunra,
Ngayakijura,
Kachingram atungli
Mamachahora.

ENGLISH:
Now the day is over,
Night is drawing nigh;
Shadows of the evening
Steal across the sky.

[STANZA 2]
TANGKHUL:
Arui tangmanghowa,
Sirala shokra,
Vano, sa, awonla,
Piyukijura.

ENGLISH:
Now the darkness gathers,
Stars begin to peep;
Birds and beasts and flowers
Soon will be asleep.

[STANZA 3]
TANGKHUL:
Jesu kakhanangli,
Laishiri aina,
Pi ngasak miluda,
Kharai ngasaklu.

ENGLISH:
Jesus, give the weary
Calm and sweet repose;
With Thy tenderest blessing
May our eyelids close.

[STANZA 4]
TANGKHUL:
Noshino mangshongli
Nali thairanu;
Makapha kasali
Lumashenrailu.

ENGLISH:
Grant to little children
Visions bright of Thee;
Guard the wandering travelers
And shelter them with peace.

[STANZA 5]
TANGKHUL:
Ngaya sailung hili
Ishamaiwui rao,
Iwui pet ngalemli
Kamayonmilu.

ENGLISH:
Through the long night watches
May Thine angels spread
Their white wings above me,
Watching round my bed.

[STANZA 6]
TANGKHUL:
Kaching kahor aina
I angkar thuida,
Nali kamataida
Otram ngathara.

ENGLISH:
When the morning wakens,
Then may I arise
Pure, and fresh, and sinless
In Thy holy eyes.

[STANZA 7]
TANGKHUL:
Varivara aina
Ano Jesula,
Mangla katharala,
Masot chikatsa.

ENGLISH:
Glory to the Father,
Glory to the Son,
And to Thee, blest Spirit,
Whilst all ages run.
