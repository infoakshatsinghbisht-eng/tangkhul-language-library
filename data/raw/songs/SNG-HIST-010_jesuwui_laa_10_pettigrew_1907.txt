[STANZA 1]
TANGKHUL:
Jesuwui mi chatsa,
Rai ngarargida;
Amaina rituwa,
Ithumla pingsa.
Jesu akavana,
Ithumli thana;
Chiaina pinglakta,
Ngararda yuisa.

ENGLISH:
Onward, Christian soldiers,
Marching as to war,
With the cross of Jesus
Going on before.
Christ the royal Master,
Leads against the foe;
Forward into battle
See His banners go!

[STANZA 2]
TANGKHUL:
Jesuwui ming shada,
Chipeena ngachee;
Arola yamthuida
Ithumna yuira.
Ithum kavao aina
Ngalai chingshong chi,
Miwui shim chithata
Shimanhorali.

ENGLISH:
At the sign of triumph
Satan's host doth flee;
On then, Christian soldiers,
On to victory!
Hell's foundations quiver
At the shout of praise;
Brothers, lift your voices,
Loud your anthems raise!

[STANZA 3]
TANGKHUL:
Jesuwui miyurna
Mi kakazip thai;
Kakharar malaima,
Ning kangarum thai.
Chipeena yuigida
Ning ngapailu ji,
Ka ning ngarumaka
Mayuirarmara.

ENGLISH:
Like a mighty army
Moves the Church of God;
Brothers, we are treading
Where the saints have trod.
We are not divided,
All one body we,
One in hope and doctrine,
One in charity.

[STANZA 4]
TANGKHUL:
Awung aina ngalai
Shimanhaila pai,
Ka Jesuwui miyur
Kashiman mavai.
Amai tui nganada,
Kapha sachingra;
Awui yurli changda
Kachingli kara.

ENGLISH:
Crowns and thrones may perish,
Kingdoms rise and wane,
But the Church of Jesus
Constant will remain.
Gates of hell can never
'Gainst that Church prevail;
We have Christ's own promise,
And that cannot fail.

[STANZA 5]
TANGKHUL:
Chiaina achangsa,
Awui yur kaphai;
Ithum aina nathum
La sa ngaroksa.
Ithum akavali,
Masot chikatsa;
A awor sathaida
Makaphali yui.

ENGLISH:
Onward then, ye people,
Join our happy throng,
Blend with ours your voices
In the triumph song.
Glory, laud, and honor
Unto Christ the King,
This through countless ages
Men and angels sing.
