HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Compiled by British frontier administrator G. H. Damant and published posthumously in the Journal of the Royal Asiatic Society of Great Britain and Ireland (New Series, Vol. 12, No. 2, 1880). Presents demographic surveys, geographic boundaries, and comparative vocabularies for hill tribes including the Tangkhul (Luhuppa). Provides crucial late 19th-century lexical data collected before the introduction of mission schools and the Roman literary standard.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Demonstrates 19th-century phonetic orthography and records customary institutional terms relating to cattle sacrifice, chieftainship, and mountain topography.
