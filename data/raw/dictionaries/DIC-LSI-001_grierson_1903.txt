HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Published in 1903 as part of Volume III, Part II of Sir George Abraham Grierson's monumental Linguistic Survey of India (LSI). Documents 241 standard comparative vocabulary items and grammatical paradigm tables elicited from native Tangkhul speakers in Ukhrul. One of the most widely cited early sources for Tibeto-Burman comparative linguistics.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Uses Roman orthography with acute accents and macrons for vowel length. Includes standard comparative numbering from 1 to 241 matching other languages across the British Indian Empire.
