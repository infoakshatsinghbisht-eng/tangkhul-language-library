HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Issued in 1995 by the Tangkhul Literature Society, this official wordbook establishes standard spelling guidelines, orthographic norms, and core vocabulary definitions for elementary and secondary schools across Manipur. It serves as an authoritative guide for authors, curriculum planners, and community translators.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Codifies standard hyphenation rules for compound verbs and prefixes, eliminating archaic spelling variants and promoting consistent orthography in modern publications.
