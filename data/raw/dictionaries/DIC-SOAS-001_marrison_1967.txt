HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Researched by British comparative linguist Geoffrey E. Marrison at the School of Oriental and African Studies (SOAS), University of London, and submitted in 1967 as a doctoral thesis. Volume II contains exhaustive comparative lexical matrices mapping hundreds of basic core vocabulary items across all major Naga groups. Establishes the phonological and lexical affinities between Tangkhul, Champhung, Maring, and the central Tibeto-Burman branch.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Systematic phonemic notation based on IPA principles with proto-Naga comparisons and reflexes for initial consonants, vowels, and final nasals and stops (-k, -t, -p, -ng, -n, -m).
