HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Initiated in 2018 by independent community researchers and mother-tongue educators as the Tangkhul Dialect Documentation Project (TDDP). Elicits everyday agricultural, domestic, anatomical, and cultural vocabulary across four geographically and historically divergent villages: Phungcham (northern), Hundung (southern valley), Champhung (western archaic variety), and Ukhrul (central standard). Demonstrates significant lexical divergences between varieties.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Preserves archaic consonants (e.g. initial r/l shifts, consonant clusters, and distinct vowel reflexes) found in Champhung and Phungcham that have merged in standard Ukhrul.

Entries extracted, normalized, and indexed with part of speech, glosses, citations, and examples:

ENTRY  1. kongrei (noun) -- great river, mighty stream [Citation: Sec. 2] [Alt: kongray]
   Context / Example: Kongreili ngashan kapam (Resting beside the mighty stream).

ENTRY  2. lengvai (noun) -- village gate, ceremonial wooden portal [Citation: Sec. 4]
   Context / Example: Lengvaili kashak ngatei (Carved markings upon the portal).

ENTRY  3. ngalungtui (noun) -- monolith speech, sacred covenant word engraved on stone [Citation: Sec. 7] [Alt: ngalung-tui]
   Context / Example: Ngalungtui thot kachunglei (Unbreakable covenant word sealed on stone).

ENTRY  4. khamor (noun) -- mouth, spoken words, voice [Citation: Sec. 1]
   Context / Example: Khamor kapha hithada hanna (Speaking gracious words in council).

ENTRY  5. shimkhon (noun) -- ancestral dwelling compound, clan courtyard [Citation: Sec. 5]
   Context / Example: Shimkhonli tui kakazip (Gathering in council at the clan compound).

ENTRY  6. ramrei (noun) -- ancestral territorial domain, sovereign village lands [Citation: Sec. 3]
   Context / Example: Ramreili rammayun (Guarding ancestral village boundaries).

ENTRY  7. luishang (noun) -- terraced agricultural slope, steep paddy field [Citation: Sec. 6]
   Context / Example: Luishangli luira kasak (Terracing the steep agricultural slope).

ENTRY  8. pheirui (noun) -- mountain path, hillside trail [Citation: Sec. 1]
   Context / Example: Pheiruili khazat (Walking upon the mountain trail).

ENTRY  9. ngaraotui (noun) -- dialectal variant, idiom of speech, local vernacular [Citation: Sec. 8]
   Context / Example: Ngaraotui eina theikhavai (Recognizing idioms of village speech).

ENTRY 10. hangvalui (noun) -- council agricultural plot dedicated to elders [Citation: Sec. 5]
   Context / Example: Hangvaluili ot kasa (Farming the council land collaboratively).
