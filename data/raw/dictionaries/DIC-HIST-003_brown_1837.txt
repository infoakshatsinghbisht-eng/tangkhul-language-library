HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Published in 1837 in the Journal of the Asiatic Society of Bengal (Vol. 6, pp. 1023-1038) by American Baptist missionary and scholar Nathan Brown. The lexical elicitations were collected by Captain George Gordon, Political Agent in Manipur, and document two distinct varieties: the northern Luhuppa (Tangkhul) and the archaic Champhung language. This is the earliest known published comparative wordlist for any Tangkhulic speech variety, predating McCulloch by 22 years.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Transcribed using early 19th-century British orientalist Roman orthography. Preserves archaic Champhung initial clusters and distinct lexical cognates before extensive 20th-century levelling.
