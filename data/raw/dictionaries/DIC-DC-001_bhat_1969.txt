HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Published in 1969 by Deccan College Postgraduate and Research Institute in Poona (Publication No. 67) by linguist D. N. S. Bhat. Consists of xii + 100 pages detailing the phonology and lexical elicitation collected from a native speaker from Hunphun (the ancestral parent village of Ukhrul). Features rigorous structuralist phonemic notation, tonal descriptions, and comparative glottal analysis.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Employs IPA structuralist phonemic notation indicating vowel quality (ǝ, ɛ, ɔ), aspirates (pʰ, tʰ, kʰ), retroflex/palatal contours, and high, mid, and low tonal registers.
