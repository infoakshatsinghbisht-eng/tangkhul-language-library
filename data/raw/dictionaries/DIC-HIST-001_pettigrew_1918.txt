HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Published in 1918 by the Assam Secretariat Printing Office in Shillong, this 476-page monumental treatise represents the first systematic grammatical description and comprehensive bilingual dictionary of the Tangkhul language. Rev. William Pettigrew, who arrived in Ukhrul in 1896, transcribed the language using the Roman alphabet and established the Ukhrul dialect as the written literary standard. Part II comprises over 2,000 Tangkhul headwords with precise English equivalents, parts of speech, and idiomatic illustrative sentences.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Uses the Latin alphabet with macron markings (ā, ē, ī, ō, ū) for vowel length and glottal/velar consonant representations (kh, ng, ch). Establishes basic inflectional patterns for prefixation (ka-, kha-) and nominal case suffixes (-li, -wui, -eina).
