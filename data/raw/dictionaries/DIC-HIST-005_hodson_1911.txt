HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Published in 1911 by Macmillan and Co. in London as the linguistic and lexicographical appendix to T. C. Hodson's seminal ethnography 'The Naga Tribes of Manipur'. Systematically compares vocabularies of Tangkhul, Mao, Maram, Kabui, and Maring across standardized semantic lists: numerals, anatomical terms, fauna, agriculture, religion, kin relations, and traditional technology.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Harmonizes phonetic transcriptions across multiple hill languages, providing an invaluable early 20th-century comparative baseline for assessing phonetic correspondence and loanwords.
