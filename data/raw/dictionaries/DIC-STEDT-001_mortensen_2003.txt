HISTORICAL & LEXICOGRAPHICAL BACKGROUND:
Developed by linguist David R. Mortensen at the University of California, Berkeley (2003/2012) and incorporated into the Sino-Tibetan Etymological Dictionary and Thesaurus (STEDT) project under Prof. James A. Matisoff. Represents the most comprehensive comparative linguistic reconstruction of the Tangkhulic language subgroup, documenting phonological sound laws, reconstructed Proto-Tangkhulic (PTk) starred forms, and multi-dialectal cognate sets across Ukhrul, Kachai, Huishu, Champhung, and Tusom varieties.

PHONOLOGICAL FRAMEWORK & ORTHOGRAPHY:
Features reconstructed Proto-Tangkhulic phonological forms (*kʰa-matʰa, *ŋa-wa, *si-ruy) and establishes rigorous tone correspondence laws across northern, southern, and central Tangkhulic lects.
