[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Faphei meikachui thalakli.' Metaphorical proverb ridiculing someone who creates theatrical drama over a trivial inconvenience.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Everyday conversation

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- Everyday speakers
- Elders
- Youth

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Colloquial idiom used to humorously defuse theatrical complaints over minor issues.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Faphei meikachui thalakli.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
Making an exaggerated fuss like a dog whose paw was touched by a flame.
