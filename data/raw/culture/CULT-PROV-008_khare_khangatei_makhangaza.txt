[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Khare khangatei makhangaza, thot khangatei maharmana.' Teaches patience in governance and personal transformation, noting that character reform takes enduring perseverance.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Generational education and governance

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- Community Leaders
- Teachers
- Elders

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Recited during educational discourses, community leadership guidance, and reform deliberations.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Khare khangatei makhangaza, thot khangatei maharmana.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
To alter an inborn habit is difficult, but to transform an entire generation requires steadfast wisdom.
