[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Khamor kachangna phei ngazekli.' Warns that harsh words inflict psychological injuries that endure far longer than physical wounds.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Social mediation and ethics

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- Elders
- Community Members

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Invoked in conflict resolution to encourage restrained, respectful speech.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Khamor kachangna phei ngazekli.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
Sharp words pierce deeper and endure longer than spear wounds.
