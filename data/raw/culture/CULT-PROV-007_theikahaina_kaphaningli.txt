[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Theikahaina kaphaningli, matheikahaina mikli.' Teaches that wise judgment looks beneath outward appearance to perceive true moral character.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Moral and judicial instruction

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- Elders
- Mentors
- Councils

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Quoted in council hearings and moral instruction to warn against superficial deception.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Theikahaina kaphaningli, matheikahaina mikli.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
The wise perceive with the discerning eye of the mind; the foolish see only with outward eyes.
