[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Ava ram shungli leishat mathamei.' A bedrock traditional saying expressing reverence and loyalty to ancestral lands.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Patriotic and commemorative occasions

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- All Tangkhul Citizens
- Community Leaders

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Recited during national assemblies, village anniversaries, and patriotic gatherings.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Ava ram shungli leishat mathamei.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
No affection exceeds deep devotion and love for the motherland.
