[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Hanna hannui shaiphamei, tuiva arartui phamei.' Teaches that while youth possesses vigor and novelty, seasoned elder counsel holds enduring guidance for navigating life.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Universal oral counsel

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- Village Elders
- Family Mentors
- Youth

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Oral proverb quoted during council discussions, family disputes, and elder mentoring.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Hanna hannui shaiphamei, tuiva arartui phamei.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
Tender young wild greens are tasty, but elder wisdom is sweeter still.
