[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Shiyan mahara ngalemli.' Drawing on indigenous metallurgical crafts, it teaches that hardships fortify moral character.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Moral encouragement during adversity

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- Mentors
- Apprentices
- Youth

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Shared as moral encouragement when facing difficult trials, sickness, or hardship.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Shiyan mahara ngalemli.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
Iron is tempered and transformed into resilient steel only by striking.
