[ETHNOGRAPHIC SUMMARY & CONTEXT]
Original saying: 'Nguihaowada masi kongra li machikhai paimana.' Emphasizes that traditional Tangkhul customary jurisprudence values restitution, reform, and reconciliation over cruel or irreversible punishment.

[SEASONAL TIMING & OCCASION]
Customary legal dispute resolution

[CEREMONIAL PARTICIPANTS & ROLES]
- Hangva Council
- Disputing Parties
- Community Mediators

[PROCEDURAL STAGES & RITUAL SEQUENCE]
Quoted in Hangva council deliberations to balance justice with compassion and restitution.

[INDIGENOUS TANGKHUL TEXT / SAYINGS / CHANTS]
Nguihaowada masi kongra li machikhai paimana.

[ENGLISH TRANSLATION & MEANING]
A person cannot be drowned in the river for committing a mere mistake.
